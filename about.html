Bookkeeping Process

An unexplained balance, a growing stack of receipts, or a month that never quite gets closed can make the financial side of business feel heavier than it should. Paper Trail focuses on the bookkeeping work that brings order to those details. That can mean rebuilding overdue months, correcting transaction records, or keeping a regular monthly process moving.

01

Record Review

Understand the source of a number before changing it. Statements, transaction history and supporting documents help establish what actually happened.

02

Financial Reporting

A report should come with context. Open items, unusual transactions and unresolved differences belong in the conversation so you know what still needs attention.

03

Monthly Records

A useful bookkeeping process fits how your business collects money, pays its bills and shares information. Clear responsibilities keep the work moving each month.
