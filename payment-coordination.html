Payment Coordination

Prepare vendor payments with an organized approval process. Paper Trail coordinates the administrative steps so your business can review upcoming obligations and make informed payment decisions.

View Accounts & Billing Packages

Payment Coordination Scope

Payment Coordination organizes vendor payment preparations for your authorized team to review. It connects pending amounts and due dates to supporting bills, required approvals and the confirmation supplied through your workflow. Paper Trail prepares and tracks the administration while your business retains financial control.

Payment Coordination Services

Payment preparation without giving up financial control

When bills are ready for payment, we help prepare the necessary information, identify deadlines, and present items for client approval. Your authorized team decides which payments proceed. Clear responsibilities help reduce confusion about pending bills and approved amounts.

What is included

  • Prepare payment details for approved vendor bills within the agreed platform or workflow.
  • Organize pending payments by vendor, amount, and due date.
  • Flag incomplete information or exceptions that require review.
  • Route payment preparations to your authorized reviewer for approval.
  • Maintain administrative records of preparation, approval status, and confirmation supplied through the approved workflow.

Approvals stay with your business

Paper Trail does not autonomously send money, release payments, sign checks, approve vendor charges, or control client banking credentials. Payment execution is performed or explicitly authorized by your business through its own approved controls.

Keep obligations visible

With clear records of bills awaiting action, approvals still needed, and payments completed, your team can identify next steps without piecing together email threads or isolated lists.

Starting with reviewed vendor bills

Payment preparation starts with vendor bills and instructions that have reached the appropriate point in your approval workflow. The vendor, invoice reference, amount and due date should be clear enough for your authorized reviewer to identify the obligation.

If a bill is disputed, incomplete or missing a required instruction, it should remain an exception rather than be presented as ready to proceed. Your business confirms the validity of the charge and the applicable approval.

The preparation list

Within the agreed platform or workflow, we organize the details of pending vendor payments for review. The list connects the proposed payment information to the supporting bill and identifies the amount, due date and approval status.

Preparing that information is an administrative task. It does not permit Paper Trail to sign a check, independently move money or release a payment. Your authorized team retains control over whether and when a payment proceeds.

Routing work to the right reviewer

Your business identifies who can approve vendor bills and who can authorize payments. The agreed workflow determines where prepared items go for review, including any need for more than one authorized person.

We organize the preparation and identify the approval still needed. A lack of response is not treated as approval. Changes to an approval path or a payment instruction need a clear direction from the responsible person in your business.

Handling exceptions before execution

A missing vendor detail, revised invoice or unclear amount can prevent an item from being ready for review. We flag the exception and identify the information needed so it is visible alongside the pending work.

Your team resolves questions about vendor identity, disputed charges and changes to payment instructions through its own controls. Paper Trail does not bypass those controls to meet a due date or decide independently which exception is acceptable.

Confirmation and administrative status

After payment execution through your approved controls, the information supplied through that workflow supports the administrative status record. Preparation, approval and confirmed payment are separate states and should remain distinguishable.

An item should not be described as paid solely because it was placed on a preparation list. If confirmation is missing or unclear, the record can identify that follow up is needed. Financial reconciliation remains part of the bookkeeping process.

What to provide and what to expect

Your team provides the vendor bill records, approval responsibilities, preparation frequency and instructions governing the workflow. Tell us about deadlines or review steps that affect how pending items should be organized.

The agreed output is a clear view of prepared items, approvals still needed, exceptions and confirmation supplied through your process. Payment Coordination supports informed client review without giving Paper Trail control of your funds, banking credentials or payment decisions.
