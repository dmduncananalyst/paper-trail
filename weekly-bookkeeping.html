Weekly Bookkeeping

Financial records maintained throughout the month.

Explore Weekly Bookkeeping Packages

Weekly bookkeeping keeps your business transactions organized on a regular schedule. Instead of waiting until month end to review all financial activity, Paper Trail reviews and updates included bookkeeping records each week. This supports current records, timely questions about missing information, and a more organized monthly closing process.

What Weekly Bookkeeping Includes

Weekly Transaction Categorization

We review and categorize business transactions each week using the information available in your bookkeeping system. Entries that need clarification are identified for follow-up.

Bank and Credit Card Activity Review

We review activity across the bank and credit card accounts included in your package, helping identify missing details, duplicate entries, and transactions that require attention.

Monthly Reconciliation

Included bank and credit card accounts are reconciled each month against available statements. Differences that cannot be resolved from the records are identified for clarification.

Monthly Financial Reporting

Paper Trail prepares a monthly Profit and Loss Statement and Balance Sheet using the books maintained for the reporting period.

Ongoing Record Organization

Regular updates help keep transactions organized, reduce month end backlogs, and make outstanding bookkeeping questions easier to identify.

Weekly Bookkeeping and Monthly Bookkeeping

Both services support organized business financial records. Weekly Bookkeeping provides a scheduled review and update each week. Monthly Bookkeeping follows the update schedule included in the customer’s selected monthly package. The standard Weekly Bookkeeping packages include monthly financial statements, not weekly financial statements.

Related Services

Bookkeeping Repair addresses records that are behind or require substantial historical correction. Accounts & Billing covers separate work such as customer invoicing, payment follow-ups, vendor bill management, and payment coordination. These services are not included automatically in Weekly Bookkeeping.
