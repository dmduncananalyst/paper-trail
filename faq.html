Working With Paper Trail

A practical look at the scope, records and communication that shape bookkeeping support.

Monthly Bookkeeping

If earlier months contain unresolved differences, bringing those periods into order helps establish reliable opening balances. The scope depends on the condition of the records, not simply the number of months behind.

Bookkeeping Software

The current system, access options and condition of your records need to be reviewed before deciding whether a change would help. Switching software alone does not correct incomplete or duplicated transactions.

Service Scope

The number of accounts, transaction volume, months involved, quality of supporting records and corrections required all affect the scope. A records review is needed before a timeline or price can be established.

Tax Preparation Boundaries

Tax ready bookkeeping organizes and reconciles the records used by your tax preparer. Tax return preparation, filing and decisions about tax treatment are separate from routine bookkeeping.
