Georgia Bookkeeping

Georgia records should preserve gross sales, delivery locations, exemption evidence and purchase tax review alongside separate Revenue withholding and Labor unemployment accounts.

Sales Tax And Purchase Review

Georgia requires a dealer to retain sales and purchase records, accounting books and invoices needed to determine sales and use tax for no less than three years. Registered filers must submit a return even when there were no sales or no tax due. Preserve the activity behind a zero return so an inactive month can be distinguished from a missed filing.

Build the sales detail from customer invoices or register exports rather than the bank deposit. Retain tax collected, returns, delivery information and documents supporting resale or other exempt treatment. The department provides ST-3 return instructions with local reporting detail. Keep transactions assigned to their actual jurisdictions and let the preparer resolve questionable sourcing or taxable services.

Sources: Georgia Revenue File And Pay Sales Tax, Georgia Revenue ST-3 Instructions

Withholding And Unemployment Documentation

Revenue identifies Form G-4 for employee withholding and G-1003 for income statement reporting. Keep certificates, pay dates, wage totals and withheld amounts linked to the employer account. Retain filing acknowledgments and compare payroll service reports with amounts actually paid to Revenue. Nonresident owner withholding is a separate issue from employee payroll.

The Department of Labor handles unemployment wage and tax reporting through its employer services. Keep the Labor account number, assigned notices, employee wage detail, quarterly reports and payment receipts together. Reconcile the unemployment balance independently of Revenue withholding, even when one payroll service withdraws cash for both. Worker classification and wages spanning several states need professional review.

Sources: Georgia Revenue Withholding Tax For Employers, Georgia Labor Employer Taxes And Claims

Vendor Bills And Income Evidence

General bookkeeping practice calls for an approved bill, evidence of receipt and payment for each supplier purchase. Capture the place the item was delivered and whether tax was charged. Flag equipment, subscriptions and supplies with uncertain tax treatment for review rather than assuming every expense from a distant vendor is exempt. Preserve vendor credits against the original purchase.

Maintain separate accounts for merchandise cost, services purchased, equipment and owner spending. For income, connect invoices, customer deposits, refunds and processor deductions to the contract or sale. If financing arrives in the operating account, attach the loan agreement and record principal separately from revenue. These distinctions improve both financial reports and the preparer handoff.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Monthly Financial Review

Reconcile banks, cards and payment processors each month, including fees, chargebacks and settlements received after the sale. Review unpaid invoices and supplier balances for errors and duplicate payments. Produce the profit and loss statement, balance sheet and a list of upcoming cash commitments. These are recommended accounting routines, rather than a Georgia filing rule.

Prepare a sales tax reconciliation from gross sales through supported exclusions to reported taxable activity and tax. Keep withholding and unemployment movement schedules beside it. Each should show accrued amounts, agency payments, credits and unresolved differences. An automatic payment does not prove that the return was accepted or that it used the correct period.

Year End And Retention

Give the tax preparer the annual trial balance, reconciliations, sales tax summaries, payroll and G-1003 support, unemployment reports, inventory counts and asset schedules. Include vendor payment detail, financing statements and owner transactions. Explain unusual refunds or changes in sales channels so the preparer can assess information reporting and income tax treatment.

The verified Georgia minimum of three years applies to sales and use tax books and records. It is not a universal period for employment documents, property basis or every federal return. Preserve records involved in an audit or disputed treatment and retain usable exports after replacing a point of sale or accounting system. Confirm other retention categories before destruction.

Sources: Georgia Revenue File And Pay Sales Tax, Georgia Revenue Withholding Tax For Employers

Corporate Income And Net Worth Evidence

Georgia distinguishes corporate income tax from corporate net worth tax. Income tax uses federal taxable income with Georgia adjustments, while net worth reporting needs balance sheet and entity information. Revenue states an LLC is subject to net worth tax only when treated as a corporation for income tax purposes. Retain the federal election and state classification rather than assessing this tax merely because the name ends in LLC.

Keep Georgia and total receipts, asset locations, capital contributions and retained earnings support for the preparer. The corporate net worth period can differ from the income tax period, so label estimates, final payments and credits by both tax type and year in monthly reconciliations. The annual package should include the opening and closing balance sheets, initial or final entity changes, federal return, apportionment data and prior net worth filings. A loss in operations does not establish that every corporate reporting duty has ended.

Sources: Georgia Revenue Corporate Income And Net Worth Tax, Georgia Revenue Net Worth Tax Questions

Unemployment Contributions And Wage Evidence

Georgia Labor uses quarterly tax and employee wage reporting for unemployment, with the employer account and assigned rate separate from Revenue withholding. Preserve the taxable wage calculation and notices that explain the contribution amount rather than reconciling only gross payroll. Each month roll employer unemployment expense and unpaid contributions forward and match payments to accepted reports. Include corrections and benefit charge correspondence in the annual file so the preparer can trace changes affecting the employer account.

Sources: Georgia Labor Employer Taxes And Claims

A Savannah Retail Example

Hypothetical example: a Savannah shop sells from its counter and ships orders to other Georgia counties. Its processor combines both channels in one payout and deducts fees. The bookkeeper retains the destination for each shipment, maps refunds to original invoices and reconciles gross sales to the payout. A new display cabinet invoice with unclear tax is placed in the purchase review file for the preparer.
