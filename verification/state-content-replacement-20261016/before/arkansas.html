Arkansas Bookkeeping

Arkansas bookkeeping connects DFA tax records with separate workforce reporting. City and county sales/use detail, payroll confirmations and a clear audit trail help explain the numbers.

DFA Registration And Local Tax Records

Arkansas DFA's sales/use section issues tax permits and administers state sales/use together with city and county local option taxes and other specified programs. Keep each account, business location, legal name, ownership change and assigned filing schedule in one register. A change of activity can require more review than simply editing a website description.

Retain the permit and agency acknowledgments, not just a login. ATAP provides an access point for tax accounts, but it does not decide the treatment of every sale. Give the qualified preparer product descriptions, delivery destinations and employee locations for nexus and account review.

Sources: Arkansas DFA Sales And Use Tax

Sales, Exemptions And Vendor Purchases

Preserve gross sales, refunds, tax charged, location evidence and exemption support in a transaction file. Sales tax, use tax and local reporting should remain distinguishable even if a software export combines them. Use the current rules for the actual goods or services rather than assuming all business transactions qualify as resale.

For accounts payable, save invoices showing what was purchased, where used and any tax charged. DFA's FAQ identifies specific local tax issues on qualifying business purchases. Such relief is not an automatic deduction from every supplier invoice; preserve evidence and obtain preparer approval before claiming a credit or refund. Separate equipment, inventory and operating supplies.

Sources: Arkansas DFA Sales And Use Tax, Arkansas DFA Sales And Use Tax FAQs

Withholding Records And Unemployment

DFA's withholding instructions specify records including wage payment dates, employee certificates, deposits and annual reconciliation confirmations, with at least six years of state withholding records. Keep these materials with the reporting period and distinguish withholding from company wage expense. A payroll bank withdrawal alone does not prove a return was filed.

The workforce agency separately provides unemployment employer accounts and Tax 21. Covered employers pay unemployment tax on payroll quarterly. Keep rate notices, wage detail, reports, amendments and payment confirmations independently of the DFA account. Resolve worker status and multistate work with a payroll specialist rather than making an assumption from an invoice label.

Sources: Arkansas Withholding Instructions, Arkansas UI Employer Services, Arkansas Unemployment FAQs

Income Evidence And Monthly Statements

These are general accounting recommendations. Match customer receipts to invoices, separate owner funding and borrowing from sales, reconcile cards/banks and review outstanding supplier balances. Record credits against the correct original transaction so income and expenses are not understated by unexplained net entries.

Build liability schedules for state/local sales and use tax, employee withholding and employer payroll charges that apply. Each should explain beginning balance, additions, payments and ending balance. Show cash commitments for taxes and unpaid bills alongside customer aging so a profitable month is not mistaken for spendable cash.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Illustration: A Mixed Purchase Invoice

Hypothetical example: a company buys merchandise for resale and a fixture for its store on the same supplier invoice. The bookkeeper retains item descriptions, resale documentation, delivery location and amounts assigned to each purpose. The preparer reviews tax treatment by item instead of marking the whole bill exempt.

If a local tax credit or refund may apply, preserve the source invoice, payment and written analysis with the claim. Do not reduce current tax payable based on an owner's recollection of an earlier purchase. The illustration supplies no rate, eligibility conclusion or credit amount.

Sources: Arkansas DFA Sales And Use Tax FAQs

Corporate Income And Elective Entity Tax

Arkansas provides corporation income tax filing through ATAP and a separate elective entity tax for eligible partnerships, S corporations and LLCs. DFA states that this election is not available to sole proprietors, trusts, C corporations or LLCs taxed as C corporations. Preserve the federal classification and election documents. Payroll withholding and owner related tax payments must remain distinguishable even when both pass through ATAP.

The accounting file should retain income and deductions, asset schedules, Arkansas and other state activity, owner allocations and the federal return. Use a separate monthly schedule for corporate or elected entity estimates and credits and retain the preparer calculation supporting any provision. At year end hand over election evidence, payments by tax year, ownership changes, distributions and tax adjustment workpapers. Keep payments on behalf of owners traceable to each owner rather than burying them in wages or general taxes.

Sources: Arkansas DFA Business Taxpayer Services, Arkansas DFA Elective Entity Tax

Unemployment Contributions And Wage Evidence

Arkansas rule 11 CAR 1-205 requires quarterly contribution and wage reporting for employers within its scope, including employers electing reimbursement instead of regular contributions. The rule directs electronic reporting through the Workforce Services portal. Keep the reporting account type, quarterly employee wage file and contribution or reimbursement evidence. Reconcile the liability against accepted reports and actual payments rather than only the payroll provider withdrawal. Preserve corrections by their original reporting period and include the quarterly reconciliation in the annual payroll package.

Sources: Arkansas Rule 11 CAR 1-205

Year End Preparation, Retention And Industry Scope

Give the CPA or enrolled agent reconciled balances, sales/use filings, payroll annual totals, asset acquisitions/disposals, open customer and supplier items and unresolved notices. Preserve entity changes and prior tax adjustments for state income or other entity filing review. Filing duties depend on the legal entity and activity, not just the trade name.

Apply the verified six year withholding period only to its stated scope. Establish separate documented periods for sales/use, unemployment, assets and federal records; this resource does not invent those periods. Extend preservation for audits or disputes. Retail, rental, tourism and specialized sales may require different review under DFA programs. Bookkeeping support is not tax filing or legal advice.

Sources: Arkansas Withholding Instructions
