Kentucky Bookkeeping

Kentucky bookkeeping should preserve sales and purchase source documents, exemption workpapers, MyTaxes withholding records and separate unemployment filings, with local employer obligations reviewed independently.

Sales, Purchases And Exemption Support

Kentucky regulation 103 KAR 31:020 requires gross receipts, claimed deductions and purchase prices for relevant goods, digital property and services. Supporting evidence includes invoices, receipts, register records, bank statements, return workpapers and exemption data. Keep these documents connected to ledger entries so both taxed sales and exclusions can be explained.

For accounts payable, capture what was bought, whether it will be resold or used by the business, the Kentucky use location and tax charged. Preserve supplier credits and freight documents. Create a purchase review schedule for unclear tax treatment rather than inferring an exemption from a missing tax line. The preparer should determine service and digital property treatment from the contract and actual activity.

Sources: Kentucky Regulation 103 KAR 31:020 Records, Kentucky Statute KRS 139.720

Employer Withholding And Unemployment

Kentucky Revenue directs employer withholding filing and payment to MyTaxes and provides wage statement reporting options. Keep employee certificates, payroll registers, tax deductions, payment confirmations and year end statement data together. Check the applicable filing period in the current account instructions; older paper form instructions should not control current electronic procedures.

Unemployment wage and tax reports use the separate Kentucky employer system. Preserve quarterly employee wages, account correspondence, contribution calculations and accepted report copies. Keep records of employee work locations for review of possible local employer obligations. State withholding does not establish that every local registration or worker classification question has been resolved.

Sources: Kentucky Revenue Employer Payroll Withholding, Kentucky Revenue MyTaxes, Kentucky Unemployment Employer Reporting Specifications

Vendor Documentation And Income Evidence

General bookkeeping practice calls for purchase approval, a supplier bill, receiving evidence and payment allocation. Reconcile vendor statements before payment, retain disputed charges and credit memos, and document goods moved from stock into business use. Preserve equipment invoices and loan agreements so the asset and debt can be reviewed separately.

Connect customer receipts to invoices and contracts. Record gross sales before processing fees and keep advance payments distinct from borrowing and owner funding. Expense documentation should explain business purpose, especially when the bank description is vague or includes several items. Preserve inventory counts and asset disposal proceeds for the tax preparer.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Monthly Reconciliations And Management Reports

Recommended monthly reports include a profit and loss statement, balance sheet, receivable and payable aging, and inventory or project detail as appropriate. Reconcile cash, cards and processor clearing accounts, then review old reconciling items. Show upcoming payroll, supplier bills and tax payments in a cash forecast when timing is important.

Maintain distinct sales/use tax, employee withholding and unemployment schedules with opening balances, current accruals, payments and ending balances. Compare agency postings with submitted returns and explain prior period adjustments. Keep local liabilities separate when they actually apply, with their own notices and payment history.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Annual Handoff And Retention

Give the preparer annual reconciliations, sales and exemption schedules, purchase tax questions, payroll statements, unemployment filings, inventory records, asset transactions, financing balances and owner activity. Include accepted wage submissions and a list of amended periods. Identify unresolved local or employee location questions before the final annual package is closed.

KRS 139.720 requires relevant records for filers to be kept for at least four years from their creation unless Revenue authorizes earlier destruction in writing. The records regulation also ties preservation to the assessment limitation and extends preservation when the assessment period is extended. Apply both requirements; a four year anniversary alone does not establish permission to delete an open period.

Sources: Kentucky Regulation 103 KAR 31:020 Records, Kentucky Statute KRS 139.720, Kentucky Revenue Employer Payroll Withholding

Corporate Income And Limited Liability Entity Tax

Kentucky distinguishes corporation income tax from the Limited Liability Entity Tax, or LLET. Revenue describes LLET for businesses with liability protection, subject to statutory exclusions, while sole proprietorships and general partnerships do not fall into that same category. A federal disregarded entity classification alone does not settle Kentucky LLET treatment. Preserve formation, ownership and federal classification evidence for the preparer.

LLET uses Kentucky gross receipts or gross profits, with state restrictions on allowable cost of goods sold; it is not simply the income statement net profit. Maintain Kentucky and total receipts, returns, allowances and detailed production or merchandise costs. Reconcile corporation income and LLET estimates and credits separately from payroll during monthly review. At year end include the applicable calculations, state adjustments, owner information and payment confirmations so the preparer can evaluate liability and any interaction between the two taxes.

Sources: Kentucky Revenue Corporation Income And LLET, Kentucky Revenue Entity Tax Questions

Unemployment Contributions And Wage Evidence

Kentucky unemployment reporting requires quarterly employee wage and tax files through its employer system, with separate access for authorized third party administrators. Preserve the employer number, wage detail, contribution notices and filed acknowledgments. Match employer unemployment expense and the payable to posted payments during the monthly close. Retain amendments with their original quarters and include the quarterly reconciliation and unresolved notices in the annual payroll package, independently of MyTaxes employee withholding.

Sources: Kentucky Unemployment Electronic Reporting Specifications

A Lexington Mixed Purchase Example

Hypothetical example: a Lexington business orders resale stock and office equipment on one supplier invoice, then receives a credit for damaged merchandise. The bookkeeper splits the purchase purposes, attaches the credit to the original bill and preserves Kentucky delivery information for tax review. Customer sales remain recorded before card fees. Payroll is reconciled independently between MyTaxes withholding and unemployment reports.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping
