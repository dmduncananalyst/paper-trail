Virginia Bookkeeping

Virginia's tax audit decisions show why expense purchases and fixed assets belong in the purchase tax review alongside sales.

Sales And Business Purchase Evidence

Virginia business registration instructions distinguish retail dealer sales from other tax accounts. Preserve the sales invoice, goods or service description, transaction location and exemption support so the preparer can evaluate the actual activity.

A 2025 Virginia Tax determination describes an audit covering untaxed sales, general expense purchases and fixed asset purchases. Maintain a vendor purchase review for all three areas. A sales report alone cannot establish whether the business paid the correct purchase tax.

Sources: Virginia Business Registration Instructions, Virginia Tax Determination 25 3

Payroll And Annual Summary

Virginia generally requires employers paying wages to employees in the state to register, withhold, file returns and remit tax. The agency says withholding returns remain required when no tax is owed while the filing obligation applies.

Save payroll registers, employee authorizations and work locations and reconcile state withholding to remittances. Retain the annual VA-6 summary and supporting wage or information statement submissions. A payroll provider payment confirmation and a completed annual filing are separate evidence.

Sources: Virginia Withholding Tax

Payables, Income And Assets

As general bookkeeping practice, link supplier bills to purchase orders or approvals, delivery and payment. Distinguish inventory from equipment and general operating costs, and preserve credit memos against the original purchase.

Connect customer collections to invoices and keep owner funding and loans out of operating income. Maintain an asset schedule with acquisition documents, financing and disposal evidence for the preparer's depreciation and basis decisions.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Monthly Financial Reporting

Reconcile bank, card, payroll and debt balances before issuing the profit and loss statement and balance sheet. Review unpaid supplier bills and customer credits with the owner.

Compare sales tax liability activity to filed returns and payments. Add the general expense and asset purchase review to the close checklist so an untaxed equipment invoice is considered before year end rather than buried in a fixed asset total.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Hypothetical Untaxed Workstation

Hypothetical example: a Virginia business buys a workstation from a supplier that charges no tax. The bookkeeper records the asset, saves its invoice and delivery evidence and sends it for purchase tax review. Capitalizing the cost does not by itself answer whether tax applies.

Sources: Virginia Tax Determination 25 3

VEC Reports And Corporate Income

Virginia Employment Commission handles unemployment reporting separately from Virginia Tax withholding. Its employer page identifies quarterly tax and payroll reports, contribution payments and claim responses; its filing page provides FC-20 and FC-21 filing options and new employer account and rate notices. Reconcile payroll detail to each report, employer unemployment expense to the assigned calculation and the liability to payment.

Virginia corporation guidance distinguishes Form 500 from the Form 502 obligation of S corporations and identifies allocation and apportionment for multistate corporations. Keep entity elections, state activity, sales location and income adjustment support in the tax preparer package. Match estimated income tax payments to their correct entity and year, and keep owner distributions distinct from deductible compensation or corporate tax expense.

Sources: Virginia VEC Employers, Virginia VEC Tax Filing, Virginia Corporation Income Tax

Year End And Retention

Give the tax preparer reconciled statements, sales and purchase review schedules, VA-6 support, payroll totals, asset documents and financing records. Explain any missing source documents before final adjustments are entered.

Virginia's 2025 determination cites the general requirement to preserve supporting records for three years from the required filing date and the sales regulation requiring adequate dealer records for three years. Retain open dispute and longer lived property evidence as appropriate; the cited periods do not replace review of payroll and other record rules.

Sources: Virginia Tax Determination 25 3, Virginia Withholding Tax
