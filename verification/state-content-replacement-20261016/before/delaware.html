Delaware Bookkeeping

Delaware bookkeeping connects gross receipts by business activity, shipping evidence, withholding, unemployment and applicable Paid Leave balances. Ordinary business expenses do not reduce the gross receipts tax base.

Gross Receipts And Delivery Evidence

Delaware does not impose the usual retail sales tax. Its gross receipts tax instead falls on the seller and depends on business activity. Revenue explains that ordinary costs such as merchandise, labor and interest do not reduce the gross receipts tax base. Preserve gross receipts before supplier bills or processing costs are deducted, and distinguish activity categories for the preparer.

Revenue distinguishes goods shipped directly to customers outside Delaware from retail goods picked up within Delaware. Keep the customer invoice and shipping evidence together. Certain wholesale pickups have a separate Form 373 exemption process; do not apply that treatment to every customer who says the goods will leave the state. These distinctions make delivery documentation part of the accounting file.

Sources: Delaware Revenue Gross Receipts Tax Overview, Delaware Revenue Gross Receipts Tax FAQs

Employer Accounts And Paid Leave

Withholding belongs to the Division of Revenue, while unemployment reporting is a Department of Labor responsibility. Maintain employee withholding certificates, payroll registers, agency confirmations and rate correspondence by program. The Revenue employer publication requires pertinent withholding records for at least three years from filing the annual reconciliation. Its older form and filing references require comparison with current instructions.

Delaware Paid Leave became fully effective in 2026. Participating employers report hours and wages quarterly through LaborFirst, including applicable private plan employers. Keep coverage decisions, employee hours, payroll deductions, employer contributions and confirmations separately from unemployment. Do not infer an exemption merely because the business has a private policy or uses a payroll provider.

Sources: Delaware Revenue Employer Withholding Publication, Delaware Labor Employer FAQs, Delaware Labor Paid Leave Employer Resources, Delaware Labor Hours And Wage Reporting

Purchases, Payables And Operating Results

As general bookkeeping practice, pair every supplier bill with approval, receiving evidence and payment. Distinguish stock acquired for customers from equipment used by the business. Retain freight bills and credit memos so inventory cost and outstanding payables can be explained. A supplier charge should remain visible even when a processor or platform deducts it from a customer payout.

Classify customer receipts, borrowing and owner funding by their underlying documents. Review deposits and refunds against open invoices rather than treating every bank credit as sales. Track operating expenses for profitability and the income tax handoff even when those expenses do not reduce gross receipts tax. Preserve contracts for deposits and advance payments so the accounting basis can be applied consistently.

Monthly Reports And Reconciliations

A useful monthly close reconciles banks, cards and merchant clearing accounts, then reviews receivables, unpaid supplier bills and inventory adjustments. Produce a profit and loss statement and balance sheet on the agreed accounting basis. Add a cash forecast when major stock orders or payroll payments are approaching; accounting profit does not establish available cash.

Maintain a separate movement schedule for gross receipts tax, withholding, unemployment and Paid Leave where applicable. Show the opening amount, current activity, payments and closing amount. Compare submitted reports with the ledger and identify payments still clearing. Investigate a provider debit that covers several programs before assigning the whole amount to payroll expense.

Year End And Record Preservation

For the tax preparer, assemble annual receipts by activity, shipping support, payroll totals, agency payment histories, asset purchases and disposals, inventory counts, financing agreements and owner transactions. Explain why gross receipts filings differ from financial statement revenue when timing or classification differs. Keep a list of unresolved adjustments and prior filing changes.

The verified three year period concerns Delaware withholding records and starts with the annual reconciliation filing. This research did not establish one destruction date for gross receipts, corporate, unemployment and Paid Leave files. Use a document specific policy, preserve unresolved assessments and consult the preparer before deleting source records. Maintain accessible exports when changing software.

Sources: Delaware Revenue Employer Withholding Publication

Corporate Income And Owner Tax Records

Delaware Revenue requires nonexempt corporations doing business in Delaware to file corporate income returns, while recognizing different treatment for S corporation income. S corporations with Delaware source income have an information return and payments on behalf of nonresident shareholders to consider. Preserve classification elections, actual operating locations and ownership details; Delaware incorporation alone does not settle the income tax answer.

For monthly bookkeeping, separate gross receipts tax from corporate income estimates and payments made for owners. Retain receipt sourcing, state adjustments, prior credits and the preparer calculation supporting an accrual. At year end provide the federal business return, Delaware activity, owner residence and distribution records, estimates and filed acknowledgments. Corporate franchise or entity registration charges need their own records and review; the absence of ordinary sales tax does not remove those separate obligations.

Sources: Delaware Revenue Corporate Income Tax FAQs

Unemployment Contributions And Wage Evidence

Delaware Labor requires quarterly reports distinguishing total gross wages and taxable wages for unemployment. Its FAQ has inconsistent wage base figures, so use the current year assigned notice and instructions rather than copying those figures. Reconcile employer contributions to the quarterly wage file, posted agency payment and ledger payable. Keep benefit charge notices and wage corrections with the affected quarter. Annual payroll support should include those reconciliations in addition to the separate Paid Leave hours and wage files.

Sources: Delaware Labor Employer Questions

A Wilmington Order Example

Hypothetical example: a Wilmington retailer sells one order collected at its shop and another shipped by the retailer to a Maryland customer. The bookkeeper stores the pickup receipt for the first order and carrier proof for the second, while recording both sales before card fees. The preparer evaluates gross receipts reporting from those different delivery facts. Purchasing the merchandise remains an operating cost, rather than an automatic gross receipts deduction.
