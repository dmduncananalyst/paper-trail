Nebraska Bookkeeping

Nebraska reporting needs delivery evidence for sales tax and separately identified Nebraska wages in payroll records.

Sales, Delivery And Purchase Use Tax

Nebraska sales regulations generally locate a sale where the purchaser takes delivery. Keep the actual receiving address and item description with each invoice. The record rules require books, supporting documents, return workpapers and exemption evidence, including support for deductions from gross receipts.

In payables, retain invoices showing where goods or services are used and the sales tax already paid. Route purchases without tax to a use tax review schedule. Document the preparer decision and any credit for tax properly paid elsewhere rather than assuming an outside supplier means the purchase is exempt.

Sources: Nebraska Chapter 1 Sales And Use Tax

Payroll Records And Employee Statements

Nebraska withholding rules call for Nebraska wages and withholding to be stated separately from amounts for other jurisdictions on payroll records furnished to employees. Preserve work allocation information, wage registers and employee instructions so the payroll professional can support the separation.

Reconcile the annual withholding submission with periodic filings, wage statements and bank payments. Save complete, legible copies and submission confirmations. Keep unemployment obligations distinct from income withholding, and investigate payroll provider adjustments against the original pay period.

Sources: Nebraska Chapter 21 Withholding

Business Income And Entity Tax Records

Nebraska corporate income tax generally concerns entities taxed federally as corporations with Nebraska source income, subject to exceptions. S corporations and partnerships have their own reporting, and eligible entities may elect Nebraska pass through entity tax. Keep federal classification, Nebraska income and owner schedules, election documentation and state source activity available for the preparer.

An eligible entity makes a separate PTET election for each year, and the election for that year is binding. Reconcile entity payments and member credits with that specific year instead of assuming a prior election carries forward. At year end, include owner changes, nonresident information, incentive credit support and tax estimates alongside the trial balance; a bank payment does not itself establish that the return election was completed.

Sources: Nebraska Business Income Tax FAQ, Nebraska PTET FAQ

Income, Inventory And Supplier Documents

Nebraska income rules require records sufficient to establish gross income, deductions and credits. A farm related business should preserve beginning stock, purchases, sales and count adjustments alongside the general ledger. Keep any claim for a special treatment attached to the actual item and business use.

As a practical control, identify financing receipts and owner funds separately from operating revenue. Vendor bills should connect to purchase approval, receiving evidence and payment. Track asset acquisitions and improvements independently from routine repairs for the tax preparer decision.

Sources: Nebraska Chapter 22 Income Tax

Unemployment Contributions And Wage Reports

Nebraska Department of Labor administers unemployment tax accounts and provides NEworks functions for tax and wage reports and payments. Keep the NDOL account, employee wage detail and contribution notices separate from Department of Revenue withholding records. Save the accepted report and payment confirmation for each required period.

Compare reported wages with payroll, preserve the reason for any coverage or taxable wage difference and reconcile contributions to the ledger. Maintain an amendment log that identifies the affected workers and quarter. Give the preparer the year end unemployment balance and outstanding tax notices as well as the wage history needed to review the next contribution rate.

Sources: Nebraska Unemployment Tax

Monthly Reporting And Use Tax Review

A monthly close should reconcile cash, credit cards, customer balances, unpaid bills and debt before presenting financial statements. Review deposits for future deliveries and unrecorded supplier bills with the owner. Explain material seasonality in the income statement and cash needs.

Keep a purchase tax review register with vendor, use location, amount, tax already paid and decision. Compare the completed tax workpapers with ledger liabilities and filing acknowledgments. An unresolved purchase should remain visible even when the bank reconciles.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Hypothetical Delivery Change

Hypothetical example: a Nebraska equipment dealer accepts an order for customer pickup, but later delivers it to a different Nebraska location. The bookkeeper retains the changed order and proof of delivery and asks the preparer to review the tax sourcing before final reporting. A billing address alone is not substituted for the actual transaction facts.

Sources: Nebraska Chapter 1 Sales And Use Tax

Year End And Distinct Retention Rules

Prepare the handoff with sales by delivery location, exemption support, purchase tax decisions, inventory adjustments, assets, loans and payroll reconciliations. Include open deficiency notices and unresolved credits so the preparer knows which periods remain unsettled.

Sales rule 008.07 requires at least three years after filing and preservation while relevant refund or redetermination matters remain pending. Withholding rule 013.05 requires employer filed forms and employee documents for at least three years, with unresolved notices extending preservation. Income records remain needed while material to tax administration and unresolved deficiencies. These rules do not authorize destroying property basis evidence merely because three years passed.

Sources: Nebraska Chapter 1 Sales And Use Tax, Nebraska Chapter 21 Withholding, Nebraska Chapter 22 Income Tax
