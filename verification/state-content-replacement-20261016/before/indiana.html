Indiana Bookkeeping

Indiana bookkeeping should connect sales and use tax evidence, state and county withholding, annual WH-3 reconciliation and separate DWD wage reporting. County certificates deserve their own payroll control.

Sales, Purchases And Tax Accounts

Indiana DOR describes sales and withholding filing through electronic services and issues a Registered Retail Merchant Certificate after the business tax registration process. Preserve the registration and actual account details rather than using entity formation documents as evidence that tax accounts are open.

The DOR audit manual identifies sales invoices, point of sale records, return calculations and exemption support as sales tax evidence. Its use tax discussion emphasizes retaining the purchases on which tax was reported. Keep vendor invoices, delivery locations, items purchased and tax treatment with the workpaper. A total use tax payment cannot explain which purchases it covers.

Sources: Indiana DOR Business FAQ, Indiana DOR Audit Manual

State And County Payroll Evidence

Indiana WH-3 reconciles state and county income taxes withheld during the year. Retain employee WH-4 certificates, payroll registers, tax detail and remittance confirmations so county amounts can be compared with the provider records. Review certificate changes and uncertain residency facts with the payroll professional rather than assuming a mailing address resolves county withholding.

DWD unemployment wage reports use a separate employer account and reporting process. Its current employer page announces enhanced wage fields beginning with fourth quarter 2026 reports, including job titles, hours paid and reporting unit information. Review provider readiness before that report. Preserve the wage files, filed acknowledgments and employer contribution notices independently of DOR withholding.

Sources: Indiana DOR Withholding Income, Indiana DWD Employer Resources

Vendor Documents And Income Classification

As a general accounting routine, match vendor bills with approval, delivery and payment, and use supplier statements to catch missing credits or duplicate bills. Distinguish inventory from equipment, and retain financing agreements for installment purchases. Document stock withdrawn for the business or owner instead of treating it as an unexplained inventory difference.

Match customer invoices, deposits, refunds and merchant settlements to their source transactions. Separate loans and owner funds from revenue and separate principal repayment from interest. Keep the contract for customer advances so the agreed accounting basis can be applied. If projects span months, preserve job costs and amounts billed to explain margin movements.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Monthly Close And Financial Reports

Recommended monthly reports include a reconciled profit and loss statement, balance sheet, receivable and payable aging, and project or inventory detail where useful. Reconcile cash, cards and processor balances, review stale clearing entries and investigate negative customer or vendor balances. A transfer between business accounts should not inflate sales or expenses.

Prepare separate schedules for sales/use tax, state withholding, county withholding and unemployment. Compare report totals with ledger accruals and payments, and document timing differences at month end. Retain agency credits and amendments in the same period file so the next close starts from a supported opening balance.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Annual Preparation And Retention

Deliver the annual trial balance, reconciliations, sales and use tax calculations, exemption files, WH-3 support, payroll statements and unemployment reports to the preparer. Add inventory counts, asset transactions, financing balances and owner activity. Flag missing certificates and any enhanced wage data that the provider cannot yet produce.

The current DOR business handbook says to keep use tax transaction documents, accrual records, returns and asset support for at least three years in addition to the current year, and longer for pending proceedings or appeals. This verified category is not permission to discard every payroll or property document at that point. Preserve open periods and confirm other retention requirements before deleting original exports.

Sources: Indiana DOR Audit Manual, Indiana DOR Withholding Income, Indiana DOR New And Small Business Handbook

Corporate Income And Optional Entity Tax

Indiana generally subjects C corporations doing business in the state to adjusted gross income tax. Eligible partnerships, S corporations and similarly taxed LLCs may instead have a voluntary PTET election to evaluate for their owners. Preserve the actual classification and approved election. Neither a retail certificate nor the payroll withholding account establishes that the entity has made an income tax election.

Keep Indiana adjustments, receipt sourcing, owner allocation evidence and the federal return in the preparation file. Reconcile corporate or PTET estimates separately from state and county wage withholding; use the correct tax year and preserve any composite reporting support applicable to nonresident owners. At year end provide the election, owner changes, distributions, asset differences and payment confirmations. The preparer should reconcile owner credits with the entity calculation before statements are finalized.

Sources: Indiana DOR Corporate Income Overview, Indiana DOR PTET Questions

Unemployment Contributions And Wage Evidence

DWD requires covered employers to file quarterly unemployment wage reports and directs wages to the quarter in which they were paid. Contributory and reimbursable employers generally report electronically unless a waiver is granted. Keep employee wage detail, account notices and report acceptance with payment posting evidence. Reconcile contribution expense and unpaid liabilities monthly while preserving the original quarters of corrections. The annual payroll handoff should include those reports and the enhanced reporting fields announced for fourth quarter 2026.

Sources: Indiana DWD Quarterly Reporting

A Fort Wayne Workshop Example

Hypothetical example: a Fort Wayne workshop sells finished goods and buys machinery from a supplier that did not clearly identify tax. The bookkeeper retains the machinery invoice and delivery facts for use tax review, reconciles product sales before card fees, and keeps employee WH-4 certificates with state and county payroll totals. The preparer resolves the machinery treatment while payroll checks the county records.
