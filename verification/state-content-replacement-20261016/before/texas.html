Texas Bookkeeping

Texas bookkeeping needs to distinguish sales/use tax reporting, franchise tax information and employer unemployment records. The useful question is which evidence supports each obligation, not whether one bank reconciliation proves the business is tax ready.

Texas Tax Accounts And Filing Responsibilities

Maintain separate records of Comptroller sales tax permits, franchise tax accounts, TWC unemployment registration where applicable and the people responsible for each filing. Record the legal entity, federal identification number, location history and notices. A federal tax election does not by itself decide Texas franchise tax status, and a sales permit does not establish that every service offered is taxable.

Texas franchise tax concerns taxable entities formed or doing business in Texas, subject to statutory exceptions. Beginning with reports due in 2024, entities at or below the no tax due threshold generally no longer file a Franchise Tax Report but still have an annual information report requirement. Verify the current report year's instructions and entity facts rather than deleting the annual task because the expected tax payment is zero. No threshold amount is asserted in this resource.

Sources: Texas Comptroller — Sales And Use Tax, Texas Comptroller — Franchise Tax Overview

Sales And Use Tax Evidence

Texas taxes most retail goods and specified services, and local tax jurisdictions matter. Preserve what was sold, the customer's delivery location, date, tax charged, returns and supporting certificates. Build a sales system to ledger reconciliation that retains gross receipts even when a marketplace or processor settles only a net amount. Keep marketplace documentation separate from direct sale records so the preparer can determine collection and reporting responsibilities.

On the purchase side, identify taxable items used in Texas for which the vendor did not charge tax. Keep the invoice, delivery/use location and any exemption or resale basis together. A vendor bill with no tax is a review item, not automatic permission to ignore use tax. The Comptroller assigns reporting frequency; preserve the assignment notice and use the current calendar rather than assuming all businesses file monthly.

Sources: Texas Comptroller — Sales And Use Tax, Texas Comptroller — Keeping Sales And Use Tax Records

Service Classification And Vendor Bills

Texas provides an especially important distinction for service businesses: taxable data processing can include entering or manipulating customer data, while using a computer to apply accounting knowledge and prepare financial statements is described differently by the Comptroller. That does not establish the treatment of every bookkeeping package. Scope, bundling and what is actually delivered matter, including when taxable and unrelated nontaxable work share an invoice.

Keep contracts and invoices descriptive enough for a qualified adviser to review each component. Avoid applying a blanket 'all professional services exempt' label or treating any software assisted work as taxable data processing. For accounts payable, request corrected invoices or classification advice when treatment is unclear; preserve the original and correction. This resource gives no percentage allocation or service tax conclusion for Paper Trail or any particular client.

Sources: Texas Comptroller — Taxable Services

Payroll And Unemployment Documentation

For employers subject to Texas unemployment tax, keep worker level wage records, payroll registers, employer rate notices, quarterly reports, amendments and payment confirmations. Reconcile wages and taxable wage calculations with the provider's output before comparing the liability ledger to TWC. An employee's net paycheck does not explain gross wages or the employer's unemployment expense.

TWC's employer guidance distinguishes employees from independent contractors and identifies covered, exempt and reimbursing arrangements. Retain contracts and evidence about how work is performed, but send classification questions to a payroll specialist or employment attorney. Do not assume that contractor treatment follows from a payment method or an agreement's heading. TWC's general recordkeeping guidance specifies at least four years for unemployment tax and employee compensation records; other employment records have separate requirements.

Sources: TWC — General Recordkeeping Requirements, TWC — How UI Claims Affect Employers

Income, Expenses And Franchise Tax Handoff

Keep customer revenue, refunds, loans, owner contributions and transfers identifiable. For a multistate seller, preserve records that let the preparer examine Texas receipts and activity without guessing from a bank address. Track costs by a meaningful business category, but do not assume the ledger's ordinary cost of goods sold account equals the amount allowed in a franchise tax computation.

At year end, provide the preparer with ownership and entity changes, trial balance, revenue detail, payroll/compensation schedules, asset records, earlier franchise filings and prior adjustments. The 2026 forms are available separately from older report year forms. The ordinary annual franchise deadline is May 15, shifted when it falls on a weekend or legal holiday; extension, final report and special case requirements need review. Keep the information report and tax payment responsibilities separately assigned.

Sources: Texas Comptroller — Franchise Tax Overview, Texas Comptroller — Franchise Tax Forms

Monthly Reports And Cash Commitments

These are general bookkeeping practices rather than Texas only accounting standards. Reconcile bank and card balances, review customer aging, match deposits to invoices and examine unpaid supplier bills. Show owner spending and financing outside ordinary operating income where appropriate. Prepare financial statements on a consistent agreed basis so a month to month comparison means something.

Use separate schedules for sales/use tax, payroll withholdings and employer charges. A tax remittance is not a second operating expense if the liability was already recorded. Identify payments in transit and old balances that need follow up. For cash planning, list approved vendor payments and upcoming agency remittances alongside expected customer receipts; a profitable income statement does not establish that cash is available to pay them.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Illustration: Direct Sales And Marketplace Settlements

Hypothetical example: a Texas maker sells products through its own website and a marketplace. Its bank receives two deposits after fees. The bookkeeper retains the platform reports, delivery details, refunds and marketplace tax documentation, then reconciles each settlement to the gross activity. The direct and marketplace streams remain distinguishable for the sales tax preparer.

The same business purchases equipment from another seller without a tax line. Rather than treating it as resale inventory, the bookkeeper records the asset purchase and adds the invoice to the use tax review list. The hypothetical assumes neither a particular tax rate nor that the marketplace removes every reporting duty. Its purpose is to preserve the information needed for a defensible decision.

Sources: Texas Comptroller — Keeping Sales And Use Tax Records

Retention, Industry Limits And Review

The Comptroller requires sales/use records for at least four years unless it authorizes earlier destruction; retain relevant audit, appeal or refund records until the matter is resolved. Preserve certificates, transaction detail and purchase evidence, not only annual summaries. Before replacing software, test that old invoices and tax coding remain retrievable and understandable.

A retailer, data processing provider, restaurant or construction contractor can have materially different tax questions. This pilot does not decide construction contract treatment, local sourcing, exemptions, remote seller nexus or combined franchise reporting. A CPA or enrolled agent should review filing positions; TWC or qualified employment counsel should address worker classification. Paper Trail can organize and reconcile the records without representing that bookkeeping itself guarantees compliance.

Sources: Texas Comptroller — Keeping Sales And Use Tax Records, TWC — General Recordkeeping Requirements
