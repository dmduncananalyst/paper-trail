Oregon Bookkeeping

Oregon has no general sales tax, while payroll reporting can contain several separate state programs. Preserve enough detail to explain each liability.

Sales And Supplier Documentation

Oregon's Secretary of State explains that Oregon has no sales tax while other state and local taxes apply. Preserve sales by customer location and activity so the preparer can evaluate taxes outside the general sales tax system and obligations in other states.

For accounts payable, retain vendor bills, shipment destinations and tax charged by another state. A supplier charge is not automatically refundable because the purchaser is based in Oregon. Keep inventory, equipment and operating purchases separate and send uncertain treatment to the preparer.

Sources: Oregon Secretary Of State Relocating A Business

Two Different Transit Payroll Concepts

Oregon's Statewide Transit Tax is imposed on employee wages and the employer withholds, reports and remits it. The agency explicitly distinguishes that program from Lane and TriMet transit payroll taxes, which are imposed on employers. Give each applicable program its own ledger liability.

Preserve employee residence, actual work locations, wages and payroll deductions. Maintain the evidence used to decide whether district payroll tax applies. A single generic transit tax account can conceal whether an amount was withheld from workers or charged to the employer.

Sources: Oregon Statewide Transit Tax, Oregon Withholding And Payroll Tax

Combined Reporting And Payroll Close

The withholding and payroll page describes combined reporting through Frances Online, including withholding, unemployment, transit programs, Workers' Benefit Fund and Paid Leave Oregon contributions. Save the filed report and the payment breakdown by program.

As a general control, compare the payroll register to gross wage expense, employee deductions and employer charges. Reconcile each remittance with the correct agency period and retain annual reconciliation support. Do not infer that a payroll service bank debit proves every component was filed.

Sources: Oregon Withholding And Payroll Tax

Income And Monthly Financial Statements

Identify customer revenue separately from customer advances, financing and owner transfers. For a project business, maintain an invoice schedule that connects work completed, billed amounts and collections on the agreed accounting basis.

Close bank and card reconciliations and review inventory, unpaid bills and payroll clearing. Present a profit and loss statement, balance sheet and cash commitments. Explain any difference between payroll expense and payments caused by liabilities recorded in one month and remitted in the next.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Hypothetical Oregon Design Studio

Hypothetical example: a studio adds a worker whose duties occur at several locations. The bookkeeper documents those locations and supplies them to the payroll specialist, who evaluates the applicable district programs. The Statewide Transit Tax withholding remains separately tracked instead of being merged into employer transit expense.

Sources: Oregon Statewide Transit Tax, Oregon Withholding And Payroll Tax

Commercial Activity And Unemployment Expense

Oregon's CAT is additional to corporate income tax and applies through its own commercial activity framework. DOR distinguishes commercial receipts, excluded items and a qualifying expense subtraction. Keep a bridge from accounting revenue to reviewed Oregon activity, supported by customer destinations and exclusion documents. Preserve cost inputs and labor detail separately so the preparer can evaluate the subtraction rather than deducting all ledger expenses.

Oregon Employment Department requires quarterly Form OQ reporting and sends employer billing statements reflecting the account balance. Match those statements to unemployment expense, reported contributions, payments and the closing payable. Keep employee wage detail and each program calculation with Form OQ and respond to Frances information requests. Provide this reconciliation alongside the CAT receipts and expense bridge; neither schedule substitutes for the other.

Sources: Oregon Corporate Activity Tax, Oregon Combined Payroll Tax Report 2026, Oregon Employment Department Employer Responsibilities

Year End And Retention Decisions

Send the preparer reconciled payroll program balances, annual reporting support, sales by location, supplier invoices, asset purchases and ownership or loan transactions. Identify any unreviewed district assignment or other state sale.

The 2026 combined payroll instructions require WBF assessment records for at least four years and other covered payroll records for at least six years after required reports are filed. Apply those tax reporting periods to the relevant records while reviewing other personnel and asset requirements separately. Export filed reports and underlying payroll detail before changing providers.

Sources: Oregon Statewide Transit Tax, Oregon Withholding And Payroll Tax, Oregon Combined Payroll Tax Report 2026
