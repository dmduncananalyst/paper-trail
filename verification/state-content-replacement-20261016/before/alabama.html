Alabama Bookkeeping

Alabama records need to distinguish state tax, city and county tax, employer withholding and unemployment accounts. ONE SPOT simplifies filing access without making each liability the same.

State And Local Tax Accounts

Keep the legal entity name, federal identification number, location addresses and each tax account in an account register. Alabama's sales/use division administers several different programs, including consumer use, seller use, rental and lodging taxes. Choose accounts from the actual business activity rather than treating the sales tax registration as permission to ignore other obligations.

ONE SPOT through My Alabama Taxes supports state and local filing, including local taxes administered outside the state department. Preserve the locality and tax type on each confirmation. A single portal does not mean a city assessment can be offset against a state sales tax balance without authorization.

Sources: Alabama DOR ONE SPOT, Alabama DOR Sales And Use Division

Sales And Purchase Evidence

Alabama's record statute calls for suitable gross sales and receipts records, purchase invoices and books that permit the department to determine liability. Maintain item descriptions, gross transaction amounts, delivery information, tax charged and documentary support for exempt treatment. Marketplace reports should remain distinguishable from direct sales.

For payables, retain the vendor invoice, delivery location, purchase purpose and payment. Identify bills with no tax line for consumer use tax review. A supplier's omission does not settle the buyer's obligation. Do not assign a rate or exemption merely from a postal city name; local jurisdiction and the transaction facts need review.

Sources: Alabama DOR Sales And Use Division, Alabama Code Section 40 23 9

Withholding And Unemployment

The current department forms list separate monthly, quarterly and annual withholding forms, including the A3 annual reconciliation. Maintain employee certificates, payroll registers, payment acknowledgments and the actual reporting schedule assigned to the employer. Reconcile withheld tax separately from gross wage expense and net pay.

Unemployment reporting belongs to the labor agency rather than the income tax withholding account. Keep the unemployment account, assigned rate notices, wage reports and benefit correspondence distinct. The official new hire resource refers to the federal employer number used for quarterly reporting. Worker status, mobile workers and possible local occupational tax require a payroll professional's review.

Sources: Alabama DOR Current Forms, Alabama Labor New Hire Reporting

Receipts, Bills And Monthly Reporting

As a general bookkeeping routine, separate customer payments from loans and owner funding, match vendor payments to approved invoices and reconcile bank and card accounts. Investigate unmatched transfers before producing the balance sheet. A bank statement explains cash movement but not whether a sale was taxable or a worker was correctly classified.

Prepare an independent roll forward for each state/local tax liability and withholding account. Show opening balance, activity, payments and unresolved differences. When one electronic withdrawal pays several jurisdictions, preserve the allocation rather than posting the entire payment against whichever tax account is easiest to find.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Illustration: A Retailer With Two Locations

Hypothetical example: an Alabama retailer has two stores and receives one processor settlement for both. The bookkeeper preserves store level gross sales, returns, certificate support and settlement fees. The preparer can then evaluate the correct local treatment instead of treating the combined deposit as one location's sales.

The retailer also buys a display fixture without a tax line. Record its invoice and intended use separately from inventory for resale, then request a consumer use tax determination. This example supplies no tax rate or automatic registration conclusion.

Sources: Alabama DOR ONE SPOT

Business Privilege And Corporate Income Records

Alabama business privilege tax and corporate income tax are different obligations. Revenue explains that privilege tax can apply to corporations, limited liability entities and disregarded entities, including businesses registered in Alabama even when inactive, subject to the current small tax exemption. Corporate income tax concerns corporate taxable income attributable to Alabama. Keep federal classification elections, formation or withdrawal documents and prior returns so the preparer can determine which filings and exemptions apply.

For bookkeeping, preserve annual balance sheet support, ownership changes, Alabama receipts and receipts elsewhere, and the federal return with state adjustment workpapers. Maintain privilege tax and corporate income payments in separate schedules from payroll and sales taxes. At each monthly close, match estimates and credits to the correct tax year and record only a supported provision approved by the preparer. The annual handoff should include exemption calculations, payment confirmations and any dissolution correspondence; an idle bank account does not prove the entity has completed its obligations.

Sources: Alabama Revenue Business Privilege Tax, Alabama Revenue Corporate Income Tax

Unemployment Contributions And Wage Evidence

Workforce Alabama requires employers in its unemployment system to file a contribution and wage report for each quarter online; an annual combined report is not a substitute. Its FAQ provides a correction process and access to prior reports. Preserve employee quarterly wages, the assigned rate notice, accepted report and payment evidence. Reconcile employer contribution expense and the unpaid contribution balance separately from employee income tax withholding. Give the preparer the four quarterly reports and corrections at year end so wages can be compared with payroll totals without erasing the original quarter.

Sources: Workforce Alabama Employer FAQ

Year End, Retention And Scope

For year end, organize reconciliations, inventory support, assets, tax filings, payroll totals and unresolved notices for the CPA or enrolled agent. Preserve ownership and business changes so the adviser can assess entity filing requirements. Rental, lodging and other specialized activities require their own review rather than copying a retailer's settings.

Alabama Rule 810-3-70-.02 requires withholding records for three years from the return due date or three years from actual filing, whichever is later. Electronic records must meet the separate electronic recordkeeping rule. This verified payroll category does not establish one destruction date for sales/use, assets, federal records or open disputes. Maintain retrievable source detail and confirm longer holds before deletion.

Sources: Alabama Code Section 40 23 9, Alabama DOR Current Forms, Alabama Administrative Code Rule 810-3-70-.02
