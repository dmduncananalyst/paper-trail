Missouri Bookkeeping

Missouri active withholding accounts require returns even during zero periods, and sales evidence should survive beyond the legal minimum when audit issues remain.

Sales Records And Purchase Use Tax

Missouri sales instructions identify filed returns, working papers, sales invoices, exemption support, purchase invoices and asset schedules among the records to preserve. Keep the chart of accounts and customer references used to produce the return so totals remain understandable after a software change.

For payables, identify purchases used by the business separately from inventory for resale. Attach the supplier bill, delivery information, tax charged and payment. Uncertain tax treatment belongs on a use tax review schedule with the preparer decision recorded beside the original evidence.

Sources: Missouri Sales Tax Detailed Instructions

Active Employer Accounts

Missouri requires an active withholding account to file Form MO-941 even when no tax is due. The current agency explanation also states that Missouri does not honor reciprocity agreements. Keep residence and actual service location evidence for review rather than assuming an employee living across a border qualifies for an exemption.

Use completed Missouri withholding certificates in payroll and preserve changes. Reconcile wage registers, withholding liability, payments and annual statements. When an account closes, save the closure request and final reporting evidence; a quiet payroll period does not explain a missing filing.

Sources: Missouri Maintain Employer Withholding

Business Income And Entity Tax Records

Missouri generally follows federal LLC income tax classification. A business treated as a corporation uses corporate reporting, while an S corporation generally files an informational return and its shareholders report their shares, subject to applicable exceptions and elections. Preserve classification and election evidence, owner percentages, Missouri source activity and the federal return reconciliation for review.

Do not treat every tax payment from the business bank account as corporate income tax expense. Identify whether it concerns the entity, an owner or nonresident member requirements, and retain the preparer treatment. At year end, provide member schedules, estimated payments, credits and any notices with the financial statements so income and owner reporting remain connected.

Sources: Missouri Corporation Income FAQ

Vendor And Income Documentation

A repair business should connect each supplier invoice to parts received, parts returned and the job using them. Separate installed parts from equipment retained by the shop. Retain supplier credit correspondence when a disputed item is settled after the original month closes.

Record customer payments against the invoice ledger and label deposits for future work. Owner contributions, equipment loans and insurance recoveries need their own source evidence. The resulting income statement should not depend on the bank feed guessing what a deposit represents.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Unemployment Contributions And Wage Reports

Missouri DES requires individual wage reports each quarter. UInteract supports contribution and wage reporting and calculates total, excess and taxable wages from the submitted detail. Preserve accepted reports, payroll exports, assigned contribution rates and payments; the withholding filing at DOR does not replace the DES report.

Compare the UInteract totals with payroll and maintain a bridge explaining excess wages and corrections. Reconcile the contribution account to the ledger and retain any payment arrangement or notice with the quarter concerned. Give the preparer the annual contribution reconciliation, amended reports and remaining balance; a business unable to pay still needs to address its reporting duties.

Sources: Missouri DES Quarterly Reports

Monthly Reporting And Agency Balances

Reconcile operating accounts, credit cards, repair receivables, vendor balances and loans before producing statements. Review uncashed payroll checks and old customer credits with the owner. Explain cash collected for unfinished jobs under the accounting basis agreed with the preparer.

Maintain a liability rollforward for sales and use taxes and for each payroll tax. Compare ledger activity with MyTax Missouri filings and confirmations. A credit appearing on an agency account should be documented and reviewed before it is applied to a future return.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Hypothetical Border Employee

Hypothetical example: a Missouri repair shop hires an employee who lives in Kansas and performs work at its Missouri premises. The bookkeeper preserves the residence, work location and withholding certificate, sends the facts to payroll review and does not mark the employee exempt solely because the address is outside Missouri. No specific tax amount is assumed.

Sources: Missouri Maintain Employer Withholding

Year End And Retention

Prepare the year end handoff with sales and purchase tax reconciliations, wage statements, open job deposits, inventory counts, asset and debt schedules and unresolved customer credits. Include amended returns and the reason for every material accounting adjustment.

Missouri identifies at least three years for sales and use tax records under section 144.320. DOR recommends five years or as long as necessary to establish that no liability exists for periods under audit, and extends the same guidance to corporate tax records. Distinguish that recommendation from the minimum; establish separate employer and federal retention schedules before deleting payroll evidence.

Sources: Missouri Sales Tax Detailed Instructions, Missouri Tax Audit FAQ
