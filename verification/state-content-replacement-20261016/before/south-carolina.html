South Carolina Bookkeeping

South Carolina installation treatment depends on both customer billing and reasonable support in the books. Preserve that connection when closing the month.

Installation And Sales Records

SCDOR explains that installation incidental to a retail property sale is excluded from sales and use tax when separately stated and reasonable based on the seller's books and records. Keep the customer invoice and evidence of the installation work together.

The sales and use manual also requires separate wholesale and retail records. Use a sales classification supported by the transaction documents rather than a single untaxed revenue category covering every exclusion.

Sources: SCDOR Sales Tax, SCDOR Sales And Use Tax Manual

Purchases And Vendor Documentation

SCDOR's manual identifies purchase invoices bearing vendor names and addresses as part of required records. Add purchase approval, delivery and payment evidence as general accounts payable controls.

Distinguish goods held for sale from installation supplies, equipment and business consumption. Send untaxed purchases and unclear contracts for tax review, keeping the preparer's conclusion attached to the invoice rather than relying on a ledger label alone.

Sources: SCDOR Sales And Use Tax Manual

Employer Withholding

Employers with wages earned in South Carolina who are required to file federal withholding returns are also state withholding agents. SCDOR describes withholding, quarterly reporting and payment responsibilities. Maintain employee declarations, work locations, pay registers and remittance evidence.

Compare tax withheld with the payroll liability and filed return, separately from employer payroll expense. Nonresident contractor payments can also raise withholding questions; flag relevant vendor arrangements for a specialist before assuming payroll is the only withholding account.

Sources: SCDOR Withholding

Monthly Income And Financial Close

As general bookkeeping practice, reconcile the sales system to customer balances and cash settlements, with returns and card fees identified. Reconcile supplier balances to statements and preserve disputed invoices without deleting their history.

Issue a profit and loss statement and balance sheet after bank and card reconciliations. Include an installation revenue and cost review where relevant so the owner can see whether invoice descriptions and supporting job records agree.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Hypothetical Appliance Installation

Hypothetical example: a South Carolina retailer sells an appliance with installation. The bookkeeper saves the separately stated invoice, technician hours and job cost support for the preparer's reasonableness review. Merely moving part of a bundled invoice into an installation ledger account would not supply that evidence.

Sources: SCDOR Sales Tax

SUITS And Business Income Records

South Carolina DEW uses SUITS for unemployment wage reports, payments, liability determinations and contribution rate history. Its wage page identifies bonuses, commissions, tips and certain other payments as reportable wages. Reconcile those payments to payroll records and retain the specialist's explanation of exclusions before matching taxable wages and contribution expense to the report.

SCDOR's S corporation page describes SC1120S, an annual license fee and an available annual election for eligible active trade or business income. Keep capital and paid in surplus records, shareholder allocations and the election decision with the tax handoff. Show income tax, license fees and payroll liabilities separately; a shareholder distribution should not silently reduce operating expense or the capital base used by the preparer.

Sources: South Carolina DEW SUITS, South Carolina DEW Wage Reports, SCDOR S Corporation

Year End And Retention

Give the preparer the wholesale and retail bridge, installation support, purchase review, payroll totals, asset purchases and filed return copies. Identify exceptions where a customer invoice and internal job record do not agree.

SCDOR's September 2025 sales and use manual states that records must be kept for four years, citing Regulation 117.200.1. Apply that instruction to the covered records and confirm longer preservation for open audits, property support and other record categories.

Sources: SCDOR Sales And Use Tax Manual
