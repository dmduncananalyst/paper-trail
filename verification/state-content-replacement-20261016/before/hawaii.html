Hawaii Bookkeeping

Hawaii bookkeeping needs gross activity classified for general excise tax, import purchase evidence for use tax, and separate payroll, unemployment and employee insurance records.

General Excise And Use Tax Records

Hawaii general excise tax is a business activity tax rather than a conventional retail sales tax. The current state brochure describes periodic G-45 returns and an annual G-49 reconciliation. Organize gross income by business activity, preserve claimed exemption evidence and keep location detail needed for county allocation. A single total of bank deposits cannot establish those categories.

The same brochure addresses use tax on imports. Retain supplier invoices, freight documents, what was imported and how the business used it. Classify stock for resale separately from equipment and supplies for internal use, then give uncertain treatment to the preparer. A charge described as tax on a supplier bill needs identification before being treated as satisfaction of Hawaii obligations.

Sources: Hawaii Taxation Introduction To General Excise Tax May 2025

Payroll And Employer Insurance

Keep the Hawaii withholding account, employee certificates, registers and payment confirmations distinct from the unemployment account. DOTAX lists HW-14 periodic withholding returns and wage statements. Its current forms list marks HW-3 obsolete for years beginning in 2020, although some explanatory pages still refer to it. Use the current year instructions when preparing annual submissions.

Hawaii Labor administers unemployment separately from Temporary Disability Insurance and Prepaid Health Care. Maintain hours, wages, coverage records, insurer bills and authorized deductions so a specialist can evaluate employee eligibility and the employer share. Reconcile insurance payables to insurer statements rather than including every benefit withdrawal in one tax account. The programs have different purposes and coverage conditions.

Sources: Hawaii Taxation Current Forms Listing, Hawaii Labor Employer Unemployment Questions, Hawaii Labor Temporary Disability Insurance, Hawaii Labor Prepaid Health Care

Purchases And Operating Expenses

As a practical accounting routine, match the purchase order or approval, invoice, shipping bill and payment. Island freight can make the amount paid differ from the supplier product price. Preserve the components so inventory and equipment cost can be evaluated. Keep damaged shipment credits attached to the original transaction instead of deleting the original invoice.

Track customer deposits, completed work, refunds, loans and owner contributions separately. Record processor fees using settlement detail while retaining gross customer activity. Classify rent, insurance and service costs with business purpose evidence, and hold personal transactions in an owner account for review. Profitability reporting and general excise reporting can require different presentations of the same activity.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Monthly Close And Cash Planning

Recommended monthly reports include a reconciled balance sheet, profit and loss statement, receivable and payable aging, and a cash forecast for freight, payroll and upcoming tax payments. Reconcile bank, card and processor accounts before interpreting operating margins. Investigate deposits that cover several customer orders or several business activities.

Use distinct liability schedules for general excise/use tax, withholding, unemployment and applicable insurance deductions. Compare payroll and hours with submitted reports, then compare agency and insurer payments with the ledger. Explain differences between periodic general excise returns and the annual workpaper as they arise rather than reconstructing an entire year later.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Annual Handoff And Record Retention

Prepare G-49 support from the periodic returns, gross receipts detail and exemption schedules. Give the tax preparer the annual trial balance, bank reconciliations, asset and import records, inventory counts, payroll statements, insurance reconciliations and financing agreements. Highlight corrections and unresolved island or activity allocations.

The state general excise brochure requires relevant records in English within Hawaii for at least three years, including gross income and exemption support. That requirement does not settle every payroll, insurance or property retention period. Preserve open disputes and records with continuing basis relevance, and confirm storage arrangements before relying solely on a cloud account.

Sources: Hawaii Taxation Introduction To General Excise Tax May 2025, Hawaii Taxation Current Forms Listing

Income Tax Beside General Excise Tax

Hawaii corporate income reporting is separate from general excise tax. Current N-30 instructions identify corporate filing for Hawaii source gross income, subject to exemptions, and direct corporations with a valid federal S election to N-35. DOTAX also provides an elective entity tax for qualifying partnerships and S corporations. Preserve the classification and any approved election rather than treating GET registration as the income tax determination.

Keep income and deduction detail, asset basis, Hawaii adjustments and allocation workpapers, with owner records where income passes to owners. Reconcile corporate or elected entity estimates separately from G-45 remittances during the monthly close. The annual handoff should include federal return support, state asset differences, payments by year, N-20 or N-35 evidence where relevant and the election forms. A business with little profit can still have substantial gross activity for GET, so the two calculations should remain independently traceable.

Sources: Hawaii Taxation N-30 Instructions 2025, Hawaii Taxation Elective Entity Tax

Unemployment Contributions And Wage Evidence

Hawaii Labor says liable employers must submit an unemployment report every quarter even when no workers were paid or the tax cannot be paid. Keep the liability determination, wages by employee, contribution calculation and accepted report. Reconcile the employer contribution payable independently from withheld income tax, TDI and health plan deductions, and retain evidence for a zero wage quarter. Include quarterly filings, corrections and unresolved notices in the annual payroll package so an unpaid balance is visible rather than concealed by the absence of a bank payment.

Sources: Hawaii Labor Employer Unemployment Questions

A Maui Business Example

Hypothetical example: a Maui business sells locally made products and imports display equipment from the mainland. The bookkeeper separates gross product receipts from processor fees, retains shipment and equipment invoices, and lists the import for use tax review. Employee hours and benefit deductions are reconciled independently of general excise filings. The preparer determines the applicable activities and tax treatment.
