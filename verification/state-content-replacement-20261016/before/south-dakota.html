South Dakota Bookkeeping

For South Dakota construction work, taxable gross receipts can include materials supplied by the owner. Job accounting therefore needs information that never appears as a vendor payment.

Job Records And Owner Materials

South Dakota distinguishes qualified utility projects from other construction projects. For nonqualified utility projects, its contractor excise guidance includes owner furnished material in the prime contractor's gross receipts and does not allow a deduction for subcontractor payments.

Keep a job schedule containing owner material values and the date furnished, alongside billed receipts. Preserve project specific prime contractor exemption certificates where applicable. An ordinary cash income report does not capture every element of this tax calculation.

Sources: South Dakota Contractor Excise Tax

Purchase Tax And Supplier Evidence

The contractor guidance also addresses state and municipal use tax on material where prior sales or use tax payment is undocumented. Obtain supplier invoices or owner evidence of tax paid and connect them to the particular project.

For general payables control, match invoices to delivery tickets and approvals, distinguish materials from tools and equipment, and retain subcontractor agreements and credits. Avoid treating an owner's purchase as adequately documented simply because it never passes through the contractor's bank.

Sources: South Dakota Contractor Excise Tax, South Dakota Sales And Use Tax

Employer Reemployment Accounts

South Dakota's Department of Labor and Regulation administers Reemployment Assistance. Keep the employer account notices and assigned reporting instructions with payroll records and obtain a specialist determination of applicable coverage.

As a monthly control, reconcile gross wages, employer unemployment costs and payments to wage reporting. Preserve worker locations, time records and explanations for corrected wages. A subcontractor invoice should remain subject to classification review.

Sources: South Dakota Reemployment Assistance

Income, Expense And Financial Reporting

Reconcile contract billings, customer advances and collections by job. Report subcontractor costs as costs on the agreed accounting basis while keeping the separate excise reporting schedule intact.

After cash, card, payroll and supplier reconciliations, provide a profit and loss statement and balance sheet. Include job margins and forthcoming cash needs so an excise tax adjustment is not mistaken for an unexplained change in operating revenue.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Hypothetical Owner Supplied Fixtures

Hypothetical example: a South Dakota building owner purchases fixtures directly for a nonqualified utility project. The contractor's bookkeeper obtains their value, date supplied and tax payment evidence and adds them to the tax review schedule. The owner payment creates no invented contractor cash receipt.

Sources: South Dakota Contractor Excise Tax

Quarterly Reemployment And Income Tax Scope

South Dakota's Reemployment Assistance wage reporting system accepts quarterly reports, calculates totals and supports payment and review of previous reports and benefit charges. Maintain employee gross and taxable wage detail with the rate notice, and match the report to employer contribution expense and liability. Review benefit charge statements with the owner because those records concern the employer account rather than employee withholding.

South Dakota DOR states that the state does not impose a corporate income tax, but separately requires bank franchise returns from covered financial institutions. That limited distinction does not remove federal income reporting, tax obligations elsewhere or the sales and contractor taxes discussed here. Give the preparer a complete profit and loss statement, asset basis, debt and owner transactions and identify any financial institution activity requiring specialized review.

Sources: South Dakota Reemployment Wage Reporting, South Dakota Business Taxes, South Dakota Bank Franchise Tax

Year End And Record Retention

The preparer should receive job receipts schedules, owner material support, project certificates, subcontractor totals, purchase tax review, payroll reports and equipment records with the reconciled ledger.

South Dakota DOR states that businesses subject to sales, use or contractor excise tax must keep business records for at least three years, including returns and supporting worksheets. Treat open disputes and other record categories separately before any disposal.

Sources: South Dakota DOR Recordkeeping Requirements
