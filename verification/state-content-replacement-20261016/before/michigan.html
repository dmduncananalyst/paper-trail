Michigan Bookkeeping

Michigan combined sales, use and withholding reporting makes separate ledger schedules essential even when several taxes share a return.

Sales And Exemption Evidence

Michigan Treasury requires sellers to maintain accurate sales records. Its exemption bulletin identifies the purchaser, transaction date, item, exemption type and sales amount among the required information, with registration details for applicable resale or lease claims. Preserve this support alongside the underlying invoice.

Purchases from vendors that do not collect Michigan tax can need use tax review. Maintain a purchase register showing the item, use location, tax paid elsewhere and the preparer decision. A missing tax line is a question to resolve, not permission to omit the purchase from review.

Sources: Michigan Revenue Administrative Bulletin 2024 11, Michigan Sales And Use Taxes

Employer Withholding And Reporting

Michigan generally connects employer withholding registration to the federal employer withholding requirement. Preserve employee certificates, wage details and actual work locations for payroll review, especially where reciprocity or remote work is involved. Worker classification needs facts about the relationship beyond a vendor invoice.

Michigan reports sales, use and withholding through its reporting system, while unemployment insurance belongs to the Unemployment Insurance Agency. Maintain distinct liability accounts and payment support. A single portal payment should be allocated to the relevant tax schedules rather than posted entirely to payroll expense.

Sources: Michigan Withholding Tax FAQs, Michigan Employer Unemployment Insurance

Business Income And Entity Tax Records

Michigan corporate income tax applies to applicable corporations, including entities electing federal corporate treatment, while an eligible partnership or S corporation may elect Michigan flow through entity tax. Keep federal classification and any entity election with state activity, income adjustments and allocation support. Do not assume an LLC name establishes which business income tax applies.

For an electing entity, preserve the calculation and payment of tax and the credit information communicated to members. Treasury current guidance describes member credit reporting changes beginning with 2025 returns. Reconcile entity payments with member statements at year end, and keep personal estimates or distributions distinct from the entity liability.

Sources: Michigan Corporate Tax Base, Michigan Flow Through Entity Tax

Accounts Payable And Income Controls

A small manufacturer should distinguish raw materials, machinery, repair parts and general office purchases in its vendor files. The accounting description should explain how the item is used so a preparer can evaluate a claimed exemption. Retain the original bill when a supplier issues a correction.

Customer receipts should connect to invoices, deposits and credit memoranda. Record loan proceeds and owner funding separately from operating income, and preserve asset purchase and financing agreements together without combining debt principal and expense.

Unemployment Contributions And Wage Reports

Michigan UIA current employer guidance uses MiUI for unemployment tax functions, including quarterly wage and tax reports and wage adjustments. Older MiWAM instructions should not determine the current tax filing workflow. Preserve the accepted quarterly file, contribution calculation, rate notice and payment confirmation, including migrated account information.

Compare quarterly UI wages with the same employee level payroll register used for withholding, while preserving any difference in taxable wage calculation. Reconcile unemployment account balances and amended quarter activity separately from Treasury liabilities. Give the preparer evidence of accepted MiUI reports and any transition related discrepancies so a successful payroll withdrawal is not confused with a successful filing.

Sources: Michigan UIA Quarterly Reports

Monthly Close And Annual Return Bridge

Treasury assigns filing frequency and requires an annual return regardless of the periodic schedule. As a practical monthly control, maintain year to date reconciliations for each applicable tax so the annual totals can be reproduced from the books.

Review bank and card reconciliations, vendor aging, stock adjustments and customer balances before issuing financial statements. Explain timing differences between tax payments and ledger liabilities. Reconcile sales reported by the selling platform with processor deposits and any marketplace collection records.

Sources: Michigan Sales And Use Taxes

Hypothetical Resale Certificate Change

Hypothetical example: a Michigan parts distributor changes customer software and discovers that an exempt customer flag migrated without its supporting certificate. The bookkeeper retrieves the purchaser information and original exemption evidence, links it to affected invoices and sends unresolved transactions to the preparer. A software flag alone is not treated as substantiation.

Sources: Michigan Revenue Administrative Bulletin 2024 11

Year End Records And Retention

The year end package should connect annual sales, purchase use tax and withholding totals to the ledger. Include inventory count adjustments, asset disposals, wage reporting confirmations, debts and unresolved exemption questions. Preserve the version of the tax mapping used for each period.

Treasury exemption bulletin 2024 11 requires substantiating data for an exempt sale for at least four years from the date tax was due on that transaction. Apply that verified clock to exemption evidence. It is not a universal four year rule for payroll, personnel, federal income tax or property basis records; establish separate schedules before disposing of them.

Sources: Michigan Revenue Administrative Bulletin 2024 11, Michigan Sales And Use Taxes
