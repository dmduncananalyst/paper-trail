West Virginia Bookkeeping

West Virginia municipal tax can depend on the actual boundary rather than a familiar postal place name. Keep delivery and use locations visible in the records.

Municipal Sales And Use Locations

West Virginia Tax Division administers municipal sales and use taxes and provides rate and boundary tables. Its guidance warns that even a nine digit postal code need not match a municipality's boundaries. Retain the physical delivery or service location behind the tax assignment.

Municipal use tax can apply to taxable purchases used in a taxing municipality when municipal sales tax was not paid. Record where supplies and equipment are used, not only the supplier's mailing address. Ask the preparer to resolve boundary questions from the transaction facts.

Sources: West Virginia Municipal Sales And Use Tax

Supplier Bills And Customer Income

Keep the vendor invoice, delivery ticket and evidence of tax paid together. For materials moved between jobs, identify the destination and purpose so the preparer can review municipal purchase tax treatment.

As general accounting practice, match supplier credits to original bills and distinguish resale goods, consumed materials and equipment. Match customer deposits to invoices and identify borrowing and owner transfers separately from revenue.

Sources: West Virginia Municipal Sales And Use Tax

Employer Withholding

West Virginia describes withholding as employee tax deducted by the employer and remitted to the state. Its guidance includes commissions, bonuses, vacation allowances and tips among compensation categories. Preserve the wage detail needed to explain each component.

Reconcile gross wages and employee withholding with payroll registers, filed returns and payments. Record work location and employee authorization changes with effective dates, and leave worker classification or multistate treatment to the payroll specialist.

Sources: West Virginia Withholding Questions And Answers

Monthly Financial Statements

General monthly close work includes bank and card reconciliations, open receivable review, supplier statement comparison and payroll clearing review. Produce a profit and loss statement and balance sheet using the business's agreed accounting basis.

Maintain municipal tax totals by reporting location alongside state tax totals. Explain liability timing differences and unresolved delivery assignments. A clean bank reconciliation does not demonstrate that each sale was assigned to the correct municipality.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Hypothetical Boundary Delivery

Hypothetical example: a West Virginia supplier delivers identical materials to two sites sharing a postal town name. The bookkeeper preserves each street address and checks the actual municipal assignment before applying the prepared tax setup. The example assumes no rate and no automatic exemption.

Sources: West Virginia Municipal Sales And Use Tax

WorkForce Contributions And Business Income

WorkForce West Virginia requires a complete quarterly Wage and Contribution Report and provides an online employer system. Keep its account and wage records apart from Tax Division withholding. Reconcile individual wage totals and employer contributions to payroll expense, the contribution payable and payments, while retaining separation and benefit charge correspondence for the responsible reviewer.

The Tax Division describes corporation net income tax and separate or combined unitary reporting. Its pass through page addresses nonresident partner or shareholder withholding and the NRW-4 agreement. Give the preparer entity and ownership records, state activity, owner residency, income and expense adjustments and agreements. Approved owner withholding belongs in its own reconciliation, with distributions and tax payments traced rather than grouped with employee wage costs.

Sources: WorkForce West Virginia Employer Process, West Virginia Corporate Income Tax, West Virginia Pass Through Entity

Year End And Preservation

Provide municipal and state schedules, wage reports, purchase documents, asset history and filed return copies with reconciled financial statements. Highlight any supplier invoice missing proof of tax.

Tax Division TSD 450 says records supporting filed returns should remain while returns are subject to audit and describes the ordinary three year period from filing, with early returns generally treated as filed on their due date. Fraud or unfiled returns may reach further. This audit framework does not set one destruction date for payroll, property or disputed records.

Sources: West Virginia TSD 450 Audits
