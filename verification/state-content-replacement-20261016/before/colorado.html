Colorado Bookkeeping

Colorado combines state administered taxes with self collected home rule taxes. Records need to identify the actual jurisdiction and preserve separate withholding, unemployment and FAMLI reporting.

State Collected And Self Collected Jurisdictions

Colorado DOR administers state sales tax and many local taxes, but some home rule cities administer their own. DOR's state sales publication does not automatically describe those cities' rules. Keep addresses, delivery evidence, business locations and a jurisdiction register for the person preparing returns.

Use current local contacts and DOR resources to confirm accounts and assigned filing periods. A single portal or software rate table is not proof that every local obligation has been reviewed. Preserve any local registration and correspondence separately from the state account so a change in address does not erase earlier reporting history.

Sources: Colorado DOR Sales Tax Publication, Colorado DOR Local Government Sales Tax

Sales, Certificates And Use Tax Purchases

Retain gross sales, taxable and exempt detail, customer refunds, delivery destination and certificates. DOR describes taxability of goods, specified services and some mixed transactions; bundled work needs factual review. Preserve invoice components rather than assigning an exemption because the seller calls itself a service business.

For payables, identify equipment and supplies used by the business with no tax charged. Keep invoices, use locations and the reason for any claimed exemption. SUTS has distinct handling and limitations for state collected consumer use tax, according to its FAQ. Do not assume filing sales through SUTS automatically settles every purchase use tax responsibility.

Sources: Colorado DOR Sales Tax Publication, Colorado DOR SUTS FAQs

Payroll And Separate Program Evidence

Colorado DOR provides withholding registration, payment, annual reconciliation and wage statement resources. Keep employee forms, work locations, payroll registers, deposit confirmations and filed annual totals. Reconcile state withholding separately from employer expense and from unemployment.

FAMLI is another program, with employer wage reporting and premiums subject to applicable coverage rules. Save the approval documentation for any private plan and confirm who submits required summaries. A payroll provider's combined debit should be supported by program level reports. Ask a specialist to determine coverage, worker classification and any local payroll obligations.

Sources: Colorado DOR Withholding Tax, Colorado FAMLI Private Plans

Retention And Software Changes

For state and state administered sales tax, DOR specifies at least three years of necessary books and records, including resale purchase invoices and sufficient exemption evidence. That minimum does not establish retention for self collected cities, employment records, federal taxes, property basis or open disputes.

Maintain an agency specific schedule and obtain professional confirmation before deleting records. Test exports from sales and payroll systems before switching providers. Keep transaction identifiers and code explanations so an earlier exempt amount can be connected to the original invoice without reconstructing it from a summary spreadsheet.

Sources: Colorado DOR Sales Tax Publication

Monthly Reporting And A Delivery Example

General monthly practice is to reconcile banks and cards, review supplier/customer aging and explain financing and owner transactions. Maintain tax liabilities by reporting authority, with cash payments applied to the correct period. An unexplained difference between the sales system and revenue should remain on a review list.

Hypothetical example: a seller delivers orders into two Colorado cities, one self collected. The bookkeeper preserves each destination, invoice, refund and exemption document, then the preparer checks the appropriate authority and rule. The example does not assume equal local treatment or a universal rate. Processor settlement fees remain separate from gross sales.

Sources: Colorado DOR Local Government Sales Tax

Corporate Income And SALT Parity Records

Colorado corporate income tax and the SALT Parity election require their own accounting support. Revenue describes the election as an annual choice for partnerships and S corporations to pay Colorado income tax at the entity level. It is binding for the elected period, with specified owner exceptions. Preserve the signed decision, entity classification and owner details instead of assuming every partnership has made the election.

Maintain state income adjustments, Colorado source income and resident versus nonresident owner allocations for the preparer. When estimates apply, track entity payments and credits separately from personal owner estimates, sales tax and payroll. At the monthly close, reconcile payments to the correct tax year and keep the provision tied to the latest approved computation. Include the election, owner schedules, state adjustments and prior credits in the annual handoff so the entity payment and owner reporting agree.

Sources: Colorado Revenue SALT Parity Act, Colorado Revenue Corporate Income Tax

Unemployment Contributions And Wage Evidence

Colorado requires liable employers to provide quarterly wage detail and monthly employment data, with unemployment premiums handled through MyUI Employer+. Preserve the wage file, monthly counts, premium calculation and amendments as a distinct payroll workpaper. Reconcile the employer premium accrued in the ledger to accepted reports, posted payments and the assigned notice. Include those reports and the ending payable in the annual handoff, with any quarter corrected after the books closed. Keep unemployment controls separate from FAMLI contributions and state withholding.

Sources: Colorado Labor Wage Reporting

Year End And Professional Review

At year end, organize state and local filings, FAMLI and unemployment reports, wage statements, asset support, account reconciliations and unresolved notices. Record locations added or closed during the year. Give entity/election and ownership documentation to the tax professional for income tax review.

Retailers, mobile vendors, mixed service sellers and contractors can have different sourcing and licensing facts. This resource does not resolve nexus or any particular city's law. Paper Trail's bookkeeping support can preserve evidence and make reports understandable; a qualified preparer, payroll expert or attorney must decide uncertain tax and employment positions.

Sources: Colorado DOR Sales Tax Publication, Colorado DOR Withholding Tax, Colorado FAMLI Private Plans
