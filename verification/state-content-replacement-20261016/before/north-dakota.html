North Dakota Bookkeeping

Delivery and work locations connect North Dakota tax reporting to the ledger. Keep them attached to the underlying transaction.

North Dakota Sales And Purchase Review

The Office of State Tax Commissioner administers sales and use tax and provides filing access through ND TAP. Preserve sales details, tax charged, delivery addresses and exemption support with the reporting period. Review customer refunds against the original sale so tax adjustments remain traceable.

For accounts payable, connect each vendor invoice to receiving evidence, payment and the location where goods are used. Flag purchases without tax for review rather than automatically treating the vendor invoice as the final tax determination.

Sources: North Dakota Sales And Use Tax

Employer Withholding Records

North Dakota employers register for income tax withholding through ND TAP. The official guidance distinguishes residents, work performed in the state and wages paid for work elsewhere. Maintain actual residence and work location changes, withholding authorizations and payroll registers so a payroll specialist can evaluate those facts.

As a general bookkeeping control, reconcile gross wages, deductions and employer costs separately. Match withholding payments with filed reports and leave an explanation for any unpaid balance. A payroll provider withdrawal can contain several components.

Sources: North Dakota Income Tax Withholding

Income, Spending And Vendor Evidence

Keep customer invoices and deposits distinct from owner funding and borrowing. A farm supply business, for example, may receive a lender advance and customer payments on the same day; the deposit description should identify the source before posting.

For machinery or equipment, retain the purchase agreement, delivery charges, financing terms and any trade documentation. Present uncertain capitalization and personal use questions to the preparer with the original evidence.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Monthly Financial Reporting

Reconcile operating cash, credit cards and equipment debt before producing a profit and loss statement and balance sheet. Review seasonal customer balances and unpaid supplier bills with the owner. Identify deposits in transit and bills received after the close instead of burying them in miscellaneous expense.

Maintain a separate purchase tax review schedule with vendor, date, use location, amount, decision and return reference. Its unresolved items should appear in the monthly discussion even when the bank balance reconciles.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Hypothetical Equipment Delivery

Hypothetical example: a North Dakota repair shop receives a diagnostic unit from another state and sees no tax on the invoice. The bookkeeper attaches delivery evidence, records the equipment separately from repair parts and asks the preparer to resolve use tax. The example assumes no exemption, rate or automatic liability.

Sources: North Dakota Sales And Use Tax

Unemployment Reports And Owner Income

Job Service North Dakota uses UI EASY for quarterly Contribution and Wage Reports, electronic payment and account management. The system calculates total, excess and taxable wages and issues a submission confirmation. Keep that confirmation with a payroll wage bridge; reconcile the calculated contribution to employer unemployment expense, the liability and the eventual bank payment.

North Dakota's S corporation and partnership page describes Forms 60 and 58 and state treatment following the relevant federal entity classification. It also addresses nonresident owner withholding. Give the preparer owner residence and ownership changes, income and deduction detail, distributions and state activity evidence. Reconcile any approved owner withholding separately from employee withholding and document whether an owner payment is a distribution or expense.

Sources: Job Service North Dakota UI EASY, North Dakota S Corporation And Partnership Tax

Year End And Preservation

Give the tax preparer the reconciled ledger, inventory support, equipment documents, loan statements, payroll totals and ND TAP return and payment copies. Include a list of transactions still awaiting tax treatment rather than marking the year complete with unsupported entries.

North Dakota Century Code Section 57 40.2 09 requires covered invoices and other records of tangible personal property bought for resale or use to be preserved for three years and three months. This is a purchase record rule, not a universal disposal date. Maintain separate payroll and property schedules and preserve open audit or disputed filing records.

Sources: North Dakota Sales And Use Tax, North Dakota Income Tax Withholding, North Dakota Century Code Section 57 40.2 09
