Idaho Bookkeeping

Idaho bookkeeping should connect delivery and exemption support with purchase use tax review, Form 967 withholding reconciliation and a separate Labor unemployment account.

Sales, Exemptions And Purchases

The Tax Commission specifies gross receipts, untaxed sales, customer exemption identities, purchase prices and tax collected or paid among records to preserve. Delivery proof matters when goods go somewhere other than the seller location. Keep invoice detail and credit memos linked to the sale so reported revenue and tax can be reconstructed without relying on a net bank payout.

For supplier purchases, capture the product, delivery location, business purpose and tax line. Separate goods bought for resale, items rented to customers and equipment consumed by the business. Put purchases without clear tax treatment on a review schedule. Exemption certificates need a connection to actual customers and transactions rather than a folder of unrelated documents.

Sources: Idaho Tax Commission Wholesaler Recordkeeping

Payroll Reconciliation And Labor Records

Idaho Form 967 reconciles income tax withheld, payments forwarded to the Tax Commission and withholding shown on wage or information statements. The agency says the 2025 Form 967 onward is filed online through TAP. Keep employee registers and payment detail that allow those three totals to be compared; investigate corrections and missing payments rather than entering a balancing adjustment.

The Department of Labor provides a separate unemployment wage reporting and tax payment system. Preserve account correspondence, employee wage detail, filed reports and payment confirmations by quarter. Retain worker agreements and work locations for classification or multistate review. A contractor invoice or information return alone does not settle whether the worker belongs on payroll.

Sources: Idaho Tax Commission Filing Form 967, Idaho Labor Unemployment Tax Help

Vendor Balances And Operating Income

General bookkeeping practice starts each payable with a vendor invoice and evidence that goods or services were received. Match discounts and credits before payment, and reconcile recurring supplier statements to the ledger. For equipment financed by a dealer, retain both the asset invoice and financing agreement so the purchase is not reduced to a monthly expense.

Distinguish customer revenue from loan proceeds and owner funding. Match customer advances to contracts and track refunds against the original sale. A business selling products and performing work should retain descriptions of each activity for tax review and management reporting. Preserve inventory counts, writeoffs and goods removed for business or personal use.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Monthly Reports And Annual Preparation

Recommended monthly reports include the profit and loss statement, balance sheet, receivable and payable aging, and reconciled inventory where relevant. Reconcile banks, cards and processor clearing accounts first. Roll sales/use tax, withholding and unemployment balances forward separately with the opening amount, accruals, payments and ending balance.

Give the preparer annual reconciliations, sales and exemption detail, purchase tax questions, payroll and Form 967 support, unemployment reports, asset additions and disposals, financing balances and owner transactions. Include a separate list of corrections and uncertain classifications. Periodic reports should explain timing differences rather than hiding them in miscellaneous expense.

Sources: Idaho Tax Commission Filing Form 967

Retention And Usable Exports

The verified Tax Commission recordkeeping page states at least four years for sales and use records and seven years when there is no Idaho seller permit number. It also says resale or exemption certificates should be kept while the buyer remains a customer plus four years. Apply those categories individually rather than deleting all evidence after a customer leaves.

The cited page addresses wholesalers and related sales/use evidence; confirm the exact rule for the business and other tax programs before destroying records. Keep open assessment evidence and continuing property basis documents longer where needed. Export invoices, certificates, registers and return workpapers in a readable format before closing a software account.

Sources: Idaho Tax Commission Wholesaler Recordkeeping

Partnership Income And Owner Payment Choices

Idaho requires Form 65 for partnerships doing business in Idaho, including LLCs treated as partnerships. The Tax Commission describes nonresident individual owner withholding, composite reporting and signed owner agreements as different paths. Preserve owner residence, ownership percentages and the actual agreement or reporting decision; none of those payments belongs in employee payroll merely because it is called withholding.

Idaho also offers the affected business entity election, and a July 2026 agency announcement describes changed election timing. Keep current year election evidence with the preparer approval. Reconcile owner withholding, composite or elected entity tax payments by program and tax year at month end. At year end provide federal return support, Idaho source income, capital changes, owner agreements and payment histories so the preparer can produce the owner schedules and assess the current election rules.

Sources: Idaho Tax Commission Partnership Income, Idaho Tax Commission July 2026 Entity Election Changes

Unemployment Contributions And Wage Evidence

Idaho Labor requires quarterly unemployment reports even when no wages were paid and directs employers to online reporting unless paper permission is granted. Errors must be corrected in the quarter where they occurred. Use wage records and the current rate notice to support employer contribution calculations, independently of TAP income tax. During monthly reconciliation distinguish gross payroll from wages used for contributions, and match accepted filings with posted payments. The annual handoff should retain quarterly reports, amendments and benefit correspondence rather than substituting income tax withholding records for unemployment evidence.

Sources: Idaho Labor Employer Handbook November 2025

A Boise Wholesale Example

Hypothetical example: a Boise wholesaler sells to a reseller and also withdraws stock to furnish its own office. The bookkeeper stores the reseller certificate and delivery record with customer sales, while documenting the stock withdrawal and original cost separately. The preparer reviews the internal use transaction. The same month payroll withholding is compared with payments and statement totals for Form 967 support.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping
