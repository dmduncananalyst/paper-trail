Tennessee Bookkeeping

Tennessee business tax, sales tax and franchise and excise tax need different supporting schedules. Track the relevant activity without assuming one registration settles all three.

Gross Receipts And Entity Records

Tennessee business tax guidance considers business activity within counties and incorporated municipalities. Keep receipts by business location and activity, with refunds and other adjustments identified, so the preparer can evaluate applicability under current rules.

Franchise and excise tax has a separate entity and activity framework. Preserve formation documents, tax elections, ownership changes and prior filings. A business income report alone cannot establish an entity exemption or determine franchise tax.

Sources: Tennessee Business Tax, Tennessee Franchise And Excise Tax

Sales, Use Tax And Payables

Tennessee sales tax includes state and local portions. Use tax can apply to otherwise taxable products brought or shipped into the state where sales tax was not collected. Retain sale and purchase descriptions, destination evidence and exemption documentation.

As general accounts payable practice, match supplier bills to purchase approvals, receipts and payments. Separate inventory, equipment and consumption purchases and maintain a review list for invoices without tax. A supplier address outside Tennessee does not establish the final treatment.

Sources: Tennessee Sales And Use Tax

Employer Unemployment Records

Tennessee requires employers to complete online registration so unemployment liability can be determined. Keep the registration result, account notices, wage reports and assigned instructions with payroll records.

Reconcile employee gross pay, deductions, employer charges and payroll cash withdrawals separately. Retain actual work locations and classification support. Submit corrected wage facts to the payroll specialist rather than offsetting unexplained differences against current wages.

Sources: Tennessee Unemployment Insurance Tax

Monthly Income And Financial Reporting

Use customer invoices to distinguish revenue, advances and collections on the agreed accounting basis. Reconcile borrowing and owner funding separately from operating receipts, and maintain a bridge from ledger revenue to business tax review schedules.

Close bank, card, payroll and supplier reconciliations before presenting the profit and loss statement and balance sheet. Include cash commitments for payroll, vendor bills and tax liabilities so entity tax estimates can be planned without confusing them with sales tax collected.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Hypothetical New Tennessee Location

Hypothetical example: a Tennessee LLC opens a second shop in another municipality. The bookkeeper records the new location's receipts and expenses and provides its opening date to the preparer. The entity file and franchise and excise work remain separate from the business location review.

Sources: Tennessee Business Tax, Tennessee Franchise And Excise Tax

Employer Contributions And Filing Status

Tennessee's current employer FAQ directs wage reporting and amendments through Employer e-Services, replacing the prior TNPAWS and TPU systems. Preserve the employee wage detail and report summary as well as the successful submission evidence. Compare gross and taxable wages with payroll and accrue employer unemployment premiums separately from net pay and federal payroll deductions.

For the business and franchise and excise schedules already described, maintain a state adjustment file that identifies asset additions, disposals, depreciation differences and owner transactions for the preparer. Tie approved estimated payments and final return balances to distinct liability accounts. When a filing position changes, preserve the preparer's adjustment and its effective year so the owner can understand why cash tax commitments differ from reported accounting profit.

Sources: Tennessee Business Tax, Tennessee Franchise And Excise Tax, Tennessee Employer Unemployment FAQ

Year End And Retention

Deliver location receipts, entity records, sales returns, vendor review, payroll reports, fixed asset invoices and financing statements with a reconciled ledger. Show any missing returns or unresolved classifications explicitly.

The Department's sales and use manual requires dealer records for a minimum of three years from December 31 of the year the related return was filed. Records tied to an appealed assessment remain until final disposition. Unregistered activity and fraud can involve earlier periods; the rule is not a blanket disposal date.

Sources: Tennessee Sales And Use Tax Manual
