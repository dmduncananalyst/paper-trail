Wyoming Bookkeeping

Wyoming tax records should explain merchandise purchases and business consumption alongside sales. Employer registration also creates records outside the revenue tax account.

Sales And Purchase Documentation

Wyoming law requires sales tax records and invoices supporting merchandise purchased for resale. Its use tax law separately addresses records of goods stored, used or consumed in the state. Retain the item, transaction date, destination and evidence of tax paid.

The Excise Tax Division provides sales and use filing resources and official educational material. Keep the sales return workpapers and source transactions together. For vendor purchases, flag invoices without tax and give the preparer the actual use facts instead of assuming that an online seller's treatment is conclusive.

Sources: Wyoming Statutes Title 39, Wyoming Excise Tax Division

Payables And Equipment History

As general bookkeeping practice, match each vendor bill to purchase approval, receipt and payment. Keep resale stock, shop consumption and equipment separate so purchases can be reviewed meaningfully.

Maintain an asset worksheet with acquisition date, cost, invoice, serial identifier where useful, financing, business use location and disposal evidence. Preserve trade and sale documents with the original acquisition record for the preparer's basis and tax review.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Employer Registration And Payroll

Wyoming DWS directs employers to complete joint business registration so workers compensation and unemployment coverage can be determined. Its instructions recommend saving the registration summary and supporting questionnaire.

Keep coverage determinations, rate notices, worker classifications and job locations with payroll records. Reconcile wage totals and applicable employer charges to reports and payments, separately from net wages. Registration evidence should remain accessible even when a provider handles payroll.

Sources: Wyoming DWS New Employers

Monthly Income And Financial Statements

Reconcile customer invoices and collections, identify owner funding and loans and review deposits held for future work. Match inventory movements with sales and purchases rather than treating every stock purchase as an unexplained cash expense.

After bank, card, debt and payroll reconciliations, present a profit and loss statement and balance sheet. Include an asset additions schedule and purchase tax questions so a major equipment outlay is visible in both financial reporting and the preparer's review.

General bookkeeping reference. The example and monthly routine are practical illustrations, not additional state requirements.

Sources: IRS, Small Business Recordkeeping

Hypothetical Machine Replacement

Hypothetical example: a Wyoming shop replaces a machine and sells the old unit. The bookkeeper preserves both invoices, financing and delivery evidence and links the disposal to the original asset record. The preparer can review purchase and sale tax treatment without relying on the net cash difference.

Sources: Wyoming Statutes Title 39

WYUI Contributions And Annual Asset Reporting

Wyoming DWS uses WYUI for quarterly unemployment reports and amendments, tax payments and review of wages, rates, credits and benefit charges. Reconcile the report's contribution amount to employer unemployment expense and the payable, then match the payment. Keep workers compensation amounts separately identifiable even where the same employer portal is used.

Wyoming Secretary of State requires registered entities to file annual reports and pay the applicable asset based license tax or flat fee. Its official worksheet calls for assets located and employed in Wyoming and prescribes valuation distinctions for depreciable assets, depletable assets and land. Prepare the location and valuation bridge from the balance sheet with assessment support for the preparer; net equity or the month's profit is not the worksheet's total asset measure.

Sources: Wyoming DWS Unemployment Employers, Wyoming Secretary Of State Business FAQs, Wyoming Official Annual Report Worksheet

Year End And Retention

Give the preparer sales and purchase tax schedules, asset worksheets, equipment documents, payroll reports, DWS notices and debt statements with reconciled accounts.

Wyoming sales law Section 39 15 107 requires covered vendor books and resale invoices for three years. Use tax Section 39 16 110 separately requires covered books and purchase invoices for three years. Preserve records for open matters and confirm payroll and asset requirements before applying a disposal date to those categories.

Sources: Wyoming Statutes Title 39
