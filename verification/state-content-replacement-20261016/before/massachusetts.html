Massachusetts Bookkeeping

Massachusetts records need to support both taxable and exempt activity, with special attention to the work location evidence behind nonresident withholding.

Sales And Use Tax Records

Massachusetts vendors must keep complete sales and purchase records whether transactions are taxable or not. Preserve invoices, register details and the basis for exemptions. Keep sale price, tax, refund and payment processor fees separately identifiable when reconciling settlements.

For vendor purchases, retain item descriptions and destination information for use tax review. A bill for a computer, materials and a service should preserve each component so the preparer can resolve treatment from the actual purchase. Keep disputed bills and supplier credit memoranda linked.

Sources: Massachusetts Sales And Use Tax

Payroll And Work Location Support

The withholding publication requires employee identity, wage dates and amounts, withholding certificates, wage statements and filed returns. For a nonresident whose withholding depends on workdays within Massachusetts, the record retention regulation calls for payroll and attendance records establishing location or documentation of an allowed alternative method.

As a bookkeeping control, reconcile payroll liabilities separately from net pay and employer costs. If an employee changes remote work arrangements, record the effective date and preserve the supporting statement for payroll review. Assess unemployment, paid leave and other employer programs separately.

Sources: Massachusetts Regulation 830 CMR 62C.25.1, Massachusetts Withholding Tax Publication

Business Income And Entity Tax Records

Massachusetts corporate excise can involve an income measure and a separate nonincome measure or minimum, depending on the corporation. Ownership or use of property, employees and other business activity can affect jurisdiction. Preserve legal and tax classification, state activity, property balances, liabilities and the reconciliation of federal income to Massachusetts income for preparer review.

An accounting loss alone should not be used to erase a corporate excise accrual. Keep estimated excise payments, credits and assessments tied to their periods, and ask the preparer to explain which measures apply. At year end, provide the balance sheet detail behind property and net worth calculations as well as the income statement and return history.

Sources: Massachusetts Corporate Excise

Income And Payables Evidence

Keep project deposits, invoices, retainers and collection adjustments in a customer ledger that explains what has been earned under the agreed accounting basis. A bank deposit from a client may settle several invoices, so retain the remittance detail. Owner loans should remain identifiable through their agreements.

For purchases, connect the approved bill to receiving evidence and payment. Save asset installation and improvement documents with the original purchase record. Review personal charges and reimbursements with the owner before closing the expense accounts.

Unemployment Contributions And Wage Reports

Employers subject to Massachusetts unemployment insurance must file quarterly employment and wage detail reports and pay contributions. The assigned employer rate and covered wages drive the amount; the state provides Unemployment Services for Employers for reporting and payment. Save rate notices, employee wage detail, filing confirmations and the amount remitted.

Reconcile unemployment contributions separately from income withholding and any paid leave program amounts. Compare total reported wages with payroll and document differences in contribution wages. Include all quarterly reports and unresolved rate or benefit charge questions in the annual payroll file; a withholding reconciliation does not establish that the unemployment account is settled.

Sources: Massachusetts Employer Unemployment Contributions

Monthly Financial Review

A useful monthly package includes reconciled cash, customer aging, unpaid bills, a profit and loss statement and a balance sheet. Explain any material difference between invoiced revenue and cash collected. Review unearned deposits and unfinished work under the accounting method chosen with the preparer.

Keep sales tax and payroll reconciliations alongside the package, each showing beginning liability, new activity, payments and adjustments. Confirm that return corrections appear in the ledger and that old credits have documented owners and next actions.

Hypothetical Visiting Designer

Hypothetical example: a New Hampshire resident employed by a Massachusetts studio divides work between home and the studio. The bookkeeper preserves dated work location records and any signed certification used by payroll. A later correction remains attached to the original wage report. This scenario does not decide withholding eligibility or estimate the number of taxable days.

Year End And Preservation Clocks

At year end, assemble the sales reconciliation, wage statement support, asset rollforward, debt confirmations and customer deposit schedule. Preserve current and amended filings with their submission evidence so the preparer can connect the books to the tax history.

Sales and withholding publications generally specify at least three years after the return was filed or due, whichever is later. The regulation contains exceptions, including records supporting future property dispositions, which remain needed through three years after the disposition year return was due or filed, whichever is later. Fraud, missing returns, substantial understatements and proceedings can extend the relevant period.

Sources: Massachusetts Sales And Use Tax, Massachusetts Regulation 830 CMR 62C.25.1, Massachusetts Withholding Tax Publication
