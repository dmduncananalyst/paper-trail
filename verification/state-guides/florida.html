Florida Bookkeeping, Sales Tax & Reemployment Records

Florida bookkeeping should keep sales/use tax evidence, reemployment payroll records and entity tax information distinct. Recent commercial rental changes also make the occupancy period more important than the date a late payment reaches the bank.

Last Reviewed: October 16, 2026. Source review only. Professional review pending.

Florida Registration And Tax Account Records

Keep the Department of Revenue registration, legal entity details, approved activity/location information and tax account notices in an accessible file. Sales/use tax, reemployment tax and corporate income tax are separate subjects even where the same department administers them. Record who receives electronic notices and who is authorized to approve, file and pay each obligation. Formation documents alone do not establish every tax account a business needs.

Use the actual facts about products, customers, employees, entity classification and locations to ask an adviser which registrations apply. A Florida corporation, an LLC with a particular tax treatment and a sole proprietor should not be assigned identical filing tasks merely because they operate in the same city. This resource does not supply a universal registration threshold or determine whether a remote seller has Florida nexus.

Sources: Florida DOR — Sales And Use Tax, Florida DOR — Corporate Income Tax

Sales, Use Tax And Resale Support

Preserve gross sales, taxable and exempt categories, location/delivery details, returns and separately recorded tax. A marketplace deposit or card settlement can be reduced by fees, refunds or tax retained by another party; retain the underlying reports so the sales tax preparer can distinguish those items. Check current Department guidance for the activity and any applicable county discretionary sales surtax rather than using a single remembered rate.

Florida's Annual Resale Certificate concerns purchases for resale, not a general exemption for everything a business buys. Retain the current certificate or supporting verification used, the related purchase and its intended resale. For equipment, supplies and other business use purchases with no tax charged, preserve the invoice and use location for use tax review. Do not use a resale document to make an unreviewed tax decision about an item consumed by the business.

Sources: Florida DOR — Sales And Use Tax, Florida DOR — Consolidated Sales And Use Tax Return Instructions

Commercial Rent: Preserve The Occupancy Period

Florida repealed sales tax and discretionary sales surtax on covered commercial real property rentals for rental or occupancy periods beginning on or after October 1, 2025. The Department's notice explicitly separates that rule from payments for earlier periods. It also lists categories outside the repeal, including transient accommodations, vehicle parking/storage, boat docking/storage and aircraft tie down/storage. Do not generalize the change into 'all rent is exempt.'

For a landlord or tenant reviewing old books, save the lease, rent ledger, occupancy period, payment allocation and credits. A payment received after the effective date may still relate to a prior taxable period. Keep old commercial rental tax balances separate from current rent and follow Department instructions on remaining returns and later collections. This pilot does not decide whether an unusual lease or bundled charge falls within the repeal.

Sources: Florida DOR TIP 25A01-04 — Commercial Rental Repeal

Reemployment Payroll And Worker Records

Florida's unemployment related employer tax is called reemployment tax. For covered employers, preserve the assigned rate notice, employee wages, payroll registers, quarterly reports, amendments and payment confirmations. Reconcile payroll expense and employer liabilities to the supporting payroll output, then compare filings with remittances. Do not infer the assigned rate from another employer's experience.

The Department's employer resource requires true and accurate work records for five calendar years. Keep the source details behind the report, including worker identity, payment periods and supporting employment records. Classification and the treatment of officer payments can require professional review; a vendor invoice or a contractor label alone cannot resolve them. Also retain independent copies of filings: the Department moved reemployment eFile/Pay to a new system on August 24, 2026, and its notice says prior system access ended after September 30, 2026. Missing older returns may require a formal copy request.

Sources: Florida DOR — Reemployment Tax, Florida DOR — Employer Guide To Reemployment Tax, Florida DOR — Reemployment eFile And Pay

Vendor Bills, Receipts And Entity Tax Handoff

Link each payable to the vendor's legal name, invoice number, purchase description, approval, date, payment and credit adjustments. Maintain a separate queue for missing receipts, uncertain use tax and disputed bills. A tax question should remain visible until resolved, but it should not prevent the business from knowing which supplier balance is actually outstanding.

Florida corporate income/franchise tax is a separate review from sales tax and payroll. Give the qualified preparer the entity documents, elections supplied by the owner, ownership changes, trial balance, asset purchases and earlier return adjustments. Do not assume that a business has no state entity tax work because an owner expects no individual Florida income tax. This resource does not determine the tax status of an LLC, S corporation, partnership or a multistate business.

Sources: Florida DOR — Corporate Income Tax

Monthly Statements And Tax Liabilities

The accounting mechanics here are general bookkeeping practice. Reconcile bank and card accounts, investigate undeposited receipts, match customer payments to invoices and review supplier aging. Preserve financing and owner transaction explanations so cash receipts are not all treated as operating sales. Use an agreed accounting basis consistently when comparing months.

Roll each applicable tax liability forward: opening balance plus recorded activity, less payments, with adjustments and remaining balance explained. Separate collected sales tax from revenue and employer reemployment expense from employee net pay. A cash forecast can then show customer receipts, payroll, approved bills and agency payments without suggesting that the income statement is the same as cash available.

Illustration: Late Commercial Rent And A Vendor Purchase

Hypothetical example: a tenant pays an overdue September 2025 office rent installment together with a later covered rental period. The bookkeeper obtains the landlord statement and allocates the payment to each occupancy period. The qualified preparer checks the earlier period under the pre repeal rule rather than applying the payment date to everything. No tax rate is supplied or assumed.

The tenant also receives an equipment invoice without sales tax. Save the invoice, delivery location and proof of payment, distinguish the asset from office consumables, and refer the use tax question for review. These are two separate tasks: a commercial rent law change does not decide the treatment of an equipment purchase.

Sources: Florida DOR TIP 25A01-04 — Commercial Rental Repeal

Year End Close, Retention And Review

Assemble year end reconciliations, outstanding receivables/payables, payroll totals, filed sales/use and reemployment reports, agency notices and unresolved classification questions. Use the filing frequency and current instructions applicable to each account. Store confirmations and payments with the precise reporting period so an adviser can distinguish a late filing from a timing difference.

The sales/use instructions checked specify support for at least three years from the return filing date or required filing date, whichever is later; reemployment work records have the separate five calendar year requirement above. Neither is a universal permission to destroy all other records. Confirm longer needs for assets, audits, disputes, federal taxes and contracts. Retailers, accommodation operators and contractors need industry specific review. Owner approval and an independent Florida tax/payroll editorial review remain required before this draft can be published.

Sources: Florida DOR — Consolidated Sales And Use Tax Return Instructions, Florida DOR — Employer Guide To Reemployment Tax
