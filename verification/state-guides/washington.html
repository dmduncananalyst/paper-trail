Washington B&O, Sales Tax & Payroll Recordkeeping

Washington's B&O tax, retail sales tax and employer programs use different information. Keep gross receipts and activity classifications visible, and review the recent service tax changes before relying on old invoice settings.

Last Reviewed: October 16, 2026. Source review only. Professional review pending.

Washington Registration And Activity Classification

Keep the business license, Unified Business Identifier, Department of Revenue account details and employer registrations where applicable in an account register. Record actual locations, employee work locations and business activities. ESD directs businesses hiring Washington employees to apply for or update the state business license. Access to a shared sign in does not mean all agency reports have been filed.

Washington B&O tax is a gross receipts tax measured by business activity, rather than simply by the net profit on an income statement. Ordinary operating costs such as wages and materials are not general B&O deductions, although specific statutory deductions and credits may apply. Preserve revenue by relevant activity and ask the tax professional to review classifications. Do not assume that a loss on the financial statements eliminates every Washington tax obligation.

Sources: Washington DOR — B&O Combined Excise Return Instructions, Washington ESD — Quarterly Tax And Wage Reports

Retail Sales Tax And The Service Tax Changes

Washington expanded retail sales tax to certain services effective October 1, 2025 under ESSB 5814. DOR's current page also identifies exclusions and exemptions effective July 1, 2026 and ongoing legal proceedings. The agency says affected taxpayers should follow current guidance while proceedings continue. An older chart saying a service is never taxable is not adequate support for current invoice settings.

Save the contract, description of the deliverable, service dates, customer/location evidence, invoice and any reseller permit or exemption support. Classification can depend on what is actually supplied, including digital features or data processing, rather than the seller's industry label. Ask a qualified adviser or DOR to resolve uncertain arrangements. This draft intentionally does not declare all professional services taxable or exempt, or determine Paper Trail's own service classification.

Sources: Washington DOR — Services Newly Subject To Retail Sales Tax, Washington DOR — ESSB 5814 DAS Interim Guidance

Vendor Invoices And Purchase Use Tax

Review vendor bills as well as outgoing invoices. A service tax change may affect a buyer's expense or asset acquisition, not only a seller's tax collection. Preserve the invoice detail and period, identify whether tax was charged, and retain the reason for any claimed exemption. A charge with no tax belongs on a review list; it is not automatically a tax free business purchase.

For accounts payable, connect the purchase order or approval, invoice, credit, payment and delivery/use information. Do not erase an earlier bill when a vendor corrects its tax treatment. Keep the original, correction and corresponding ledger adjustment together. A tax specialist should resolve sourcing, resale and mixed service questions; the bookkeeper should make sure the decision has a retrievable evidence trail.

Sources: Washington DOR — Services Newly Subject To Retail Sales Tax, Washington Legislature — WAC 458-20-254 Recordkeeping

Payroll: Separate Reports And Separate Liabilities

ESD describes two quarterly tax/wage reports: unemployment insurance, and the combined Paid Leave/WA Cares report. Keep worker wage/hour data, provider reports, applicable rate or exemption notices, amendments and payment confirmations for each program. A payment credited to one program should not be assumed to satisfy another. Reconcile the ledger's liabilities by program.

Use a payroll specialist to determine employee coverage, wage definitions, employer contributions and any current exemption rules. No premium rates or wage bases are asserted here. For employees working in several states, preserve dates and actual work locations for review rather than assigning everything from the home address. Keep federal payroll reporting and Washington employer programs distinguishable even when the payroll provider withdraws money in one combined bank debit.

Sources: Washington ESD — Quarterly Tax And Wage Reports, Washington Paid Leave — File Quarterly Reports And Pay Premiums

Labor Records, Contractors And Retention

Washington L&I's payroll/personnel page specifies at least three years of payroll records, including hours, rates, earnings and deductions. Its separate business recordkeeping guidance identifies current year plus three previous calendar years for records within that guidance, including workers' compensation and subcontractor documentation. These are different scopes; do not present three years as sufficient for every employer record.

For DOR administered tax records, WAC 458-20-254 specifies five years. Keep gross receipts, invoices, exemption/deduction support and an understandable electronic audit trail. Before a software migration, test that transactions can still be interpreted, including any vendor or product codes. A longer federal, asset, litigation or contractual need may remain. Worker classification and subcontractor coverage should be reviewed with an appropriate payroll or legal professional; a Form 1099 alone is not a determination.

Sources: Washington L&I — Payroll And Personnel Records, Washington L&I — Recordkeeping Requirements, Washington Legislature — WAC 458-20-254 Recordkeeping

Monthly Statements And B&O Reconciliation

The following workflow is general bookkeeping practice. Reconcile cash and card accounts, review outstanding invoices and bills, clear unexplained transfers and prepare financial statements on a consistent basis. Keep gross revenue, refunds, owner funding and loans separately identifiable. Explain differences between billed revenue, cash received and amounts reported for tax instead of assuming one total serves every purpose.

For the Washington tax handoff, maintain an activity schedule that reconciles ledger revenue to the B&O reporting workpaper, with any proposed deductions identified for preparer approval. Keep sales tax collected in a liability schedule distinct from business revenue. Review tax payments against the correct reporting period and agency/program. This prevents a bank payment from becoming a duplicate expense or concealing an old unpaid balance.

Sources: Washington DOR — B&O Combined Excise Return Instructions

Illustration: An Advertising Contract And A Credit

Hypothetical example: a Washington business buys a package described as advertising, and a supplier later issues a credit. Save the contract and the specific services delivered rather than relying only on the word 'marketing.' Because DOR lists advertising among the affected service categories, send the scope and dates for current law review. No rate, exemption or sourcing result is assumed.

The bookkeeper records the approved bill and related credit, preserves any tax correction and matches the net payment. If the business also sells products through a marketplace, keep those receipts and marketplace statements separate from this vendor expense. A tax change affecting a purchased service does not explain how the company's product sales should be reported. The books should preserve both trails so the preparer can evaluate each independently.

Sources: Washington DOR — Services Newly Subject To Retail Sales Tax

Year End Preparation And Scope Of Review

At year end, gather B&O and sales/use workpapers, excise return copies, payroll program reports, confirmations, open receivables/payables, asset documentation and unresolved items. Review any activity reclassification with the preparer and retain the effective date. Keep earlier guidance and the reason for a later change, especially where an invoice crosses a law change period.

Retail, construction, professional services, digital activities and multistate operations need different factual reviews. This resource is not a comprehensive account of municipal B&O taxes, capital gains tax, future legislative changes or every industry exemption. A CPA or enrolled agent should review tax positions; payroll specialists and counsel should resolve coverage and employment issues. Paper Trail organizes the records and reconciliations without claiming state offices, licensed tax advice or guaranteed compliance.

Sources: Washington DOR — B&O Combined Excise Return Instructions, Washington DOR — Services Newly Subject To Retail Sales Tax, Washington Paid Leave — File Quarterly Reports And Pay Premiums
