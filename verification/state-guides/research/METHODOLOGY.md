# 50-state research matrix: scope and verification

Source discovery checked October 16, 2026. This is an editorial planning inventory, not a claim that all 50 guides have been fully researched, legally reviewed, approved, or published.

## What was checked

Each of the 50 states received a separate official revenue/tax agency URL and workforce/unemployment agency URL lookup: 100 initial official-source open attempts. The research tool returned readable agency content for 35 revenue hubs and 31 workforce hubs; other attempts returned access errors, explicit HTTP 403 responses, or timeouts. These outcomes are recorded separately per state. A blocked tool request does not prove a government site is broken or unavailable to normal visitors. Maryland's old revenue URL redirected to the current Comptroller site; Michigan, Oregon, Pennsylvania, West Virginia, Wisconsin, Kentucky, and Florida also returned official redirects where noted or preserved in their canonical agency hub URL.

Additional official topic pages were read for California, Colorado, Connecticut, Florida, Kentucky, Michigan, New Mexico, New York, and Texas. Topic-page reading confirms that the resource exists and concerns the named subject; it does not by itself establish that every potential claim, exemption, effective date, or business circumstance has been verified. Arizona business/withholding and Arkansas sales-tax pages were located in official search results after their home pages could not be read. Search-result discovery is not a substitute for complete source review.

The sales/use and payroll URL columns deliberately contain agency discovery hubs for states where a direct topic source has not yet been read. The `topic_url_scope` column makes this explicit. A hub is not evidence that a particular tax exists or applies universally. In states where wage income withholding has not been established in this research pass, the guide must verify the applicable payroll obligations instead of inventing a withholding requirement. Blank retention URLs are intentional research gaps. Florida, New York, and Texas have official retention resources located through official search results; they still require complete rule/exception review before publishing a numeric period.

## How to use the planned angles

`planned_distinct_angles` lists topics to investigate, not findings or universal obligations. A reference to a tax, paid-leave program, local levy, industry, or reporting form is a research question until the applicable official source, business scope, and effective date are recorded. Do not copy a possible state distinction into public copy as a verified fact merely because it appears in this planning CSV.

Every guide must independently resolve: registration; taxable and exempt transactions; resale evidence; purchase-related use or equivalent tax; nexus and sourcing where relevant; withholding if applicable; unemployment and related payroll programs; retention rules by agency/tax; entity and industry distinctions; and a clearly hypothetical example showing records and reconciliation. Ordinary accounting practices must be labeled as general practices rather than invented state-specific accounting standards.

Use the source URLs in the CSV as citations and discovery starting points. Add direct official URLs to a claim ledger as each topic is verified. Record the source's actual revision/effective date separately from the date the researcher visited it. No rates, thresholds, filing deadlines, retention periods, or legal conclusions are asserted by this matrix.

## Approval and publication gate

California, Texas, Florida, New York, and Washington are the five pilot states. Their separately developed draft guides must pass factual, editorial, duplicate-content, accessibility, link, and responsive design review and receive owner approval. The other 45 entries remain planning records; this matrix does not authorize generating 45 more public pages. No unpublished guide URL should be linked as available or added to the production sitemap. Nationwide flag names can remain accessible non-link presentations while guides await approval and publication.

No Google Translation API requests, paid services, billing changes, commits, pushes, or publishing were performed for this research inventory. All state guides remain English.
