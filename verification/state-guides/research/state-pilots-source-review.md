# Five-State Pilot Source And Editorial Review

Research date: **October 16, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles)**. These are English local drafts for California, Texas, Florida, New York and Washington. None is approved for publication, indexing or addition to the sitemap. No Google Translation API or other paid service was used.

The complete draft text and claim-to-source references are in `pilots.json`. Its `source_ids` arrays connect each section to the named official sources; sections without IDs are explicitly general bookkeeping recommendations. Agency-source inspection is not a professional tax opinion. The date records this research pass, not a claim that a CPA reviewed the drafts.

## Research Method And Scope

Each state was researched independently through official revenue/tax, workforce, labor or benefit sources. Sources were searched and inspected through the browser tool on the date above. Retrieved government content was paraphrased, not copied into the drafts. Browser retrieval errors are recorded below rather than treated as successful verification. No non-government source supports a tax or legal claim in the pilots.

Every pilot covers registrations; sales/use documentation; payroll/unemployment or related programs; payables/vendor evidence; income/expense tracking; monthly reporting; year-end handoff; agency-specific retention; material entity/industry limits; a hypothetical transaction; and professional-review boundaries. The guides deliberately differ in their emphasis and examples. No article promises comprehensive treatment of all state or local taxes.

No tax rates, monetary registration thresholds, wage bases, benefit contribution amounts, tax savings or client outcomes are invented. The few stated dates concern independently checked rule changes, reporting cadence, registration lead time or retention periods. A reader must use the applicable current agency instructions and their business facts. Accounting methods and monthly reconciliation suggestions are not presented as state-created GAAP rules.

## California

- [CDTFA sales/use overview](https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/sutprograms.htm): opened successfully. Supports retail-goods/use-tax distinctions, state/local/district components and relevant permit resources.
- [EDD employer payroll workflow](https://edd.ca.gov/en/payroll_taxes/employer-payroll-taxes/) and [reporting requirements](https://edd.ca.gov/en/payroll_taxes/Reporting_Requirements): official indexed content inspected. Supports employer account, worker reporting and filings; no calculation or deposit deadline asserted.
- [FTB recordkeeping](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/after-you-file/keeping-your-tax-records.html): official content inspected. Ordinary assessment period described as usually four years with exceptions; property basis records may need longer retention. The draft does not conflate an assessment period with a universal document-destruction rule.
- [California Tax Service Center business records](https://taxes.ca.gov/small-business-assistance-center/keeping-good-business-records/): official content inspected for source evidence and resale documentation.
- [CDTFA Publication 116 retention](https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov/formspubs/pub116/retaining-records.htm): opened successfully; minimum four years, longer audit/dispute holds, POS overwrite precautions.

Unresolved: current EDD DE 44 PDF could not be parsed because its content exceeded the browser tool's size limit. Older 2019/2020 editions appeared in results but are **not used to assert a current payroll retention rule**. District sourcing, entity tax, worker classification and nexus need qualified review. No universal claim that California professional services are taxable or exempt is made.

## Texas

- [Sales/use tax overview](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/sales/): opened successfully; goods/specified services, locality and assigned reporting frequency.
- [Franchise overview](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/publications/98-806.php): official content inspected; entity scope, report-year 2024 no-tax-due filing change, continuing information-report requirement and ordinary May 15 timing with weekend/holiday adjustment. [Current forms](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/franchise/forms/) inspected to distinguish 2026 from earlier report years.
- [Sales/use recordkeeping](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/sales/faq/records.php): opened successfully; gross records, certificates, four-year minimum and longer holds.
- [Taxable services](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/publications/96-259.php): official content inspected, especially distinction between taxable data processing and application of professional accounting knowledge. This is **not** a private-letter ruling for Paper Trail's packages. No percentage allocation, bundle treatment or tax amount is prescribed.
- [TWC general recordkeeping](https://efte.twc.texas.gov/general_recordkeeping_requirements.html) and [UI employer categories](https://efte.twc.texas.gov/how_ui_claims_affect_employers.html): official guidance inspected; four-year unemployment/compensation retention and separate employer categories/reporting.

Unresolved: exact classification, bundled deliverables, local sourcing, combined franchise reporting and worker status need business-specific professional review. An attempted direct fetch of the generic TWC unemployment-tax landing route failed; no claim depends on that failed fetch. Only inspected TWC guidance is cited in the pilot.

## Florida

- [Sales/use overview](https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/taxesfees/Pages/sales_tax.aspx), [corporate tax](https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/taxesfees/Pages/corporate.aspx) and [reemployment tax](https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/taxesfees/pages/reemployment.aspx): official agency content inspected; sales and reemployment pages also opened successfully.
- [DR-7N instructions](https://www.floridarevenue.com/Forms_library/current/dr7n.pdf): inspected official content supports Annual Resale Certificate scope and three-year retention from filing or required filing, whichever later. This is a sales/use instruction, not a universal retention rule.
- [TIP 25A01-04](https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/tips/Documents/TIP_25A01-04.pdf), issued July 24, 2025: inspected official notice. Commercial-rental repeal concerns occupancy periods beginning October 1, 2025, not every payment received after that date. Earlier periods and excluded transient/parking/boat/aircraft categories are expressly distinguished. No old rate is repeated.
- [Employer reemployment guide](https://floridarevenue.com/Forms_library/current/guides/rt800002.pdf): official content inspected for five-calendar-year work-record preservation and report corrections.
- [Reemployment eFile/Pay transition](https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/eservices/Pages/rtlogin.aspx): official current content inspected for August 24, 2026 transition, prior-system access ending September 30, 2026 and copy requests through DR-841.

Unresolved: agency `/current/` PDFs can be replaced; recheck editions before release. Lease scope, officer wages, worker classification, entity tax and industry-specific obligations need professional review. The payroll-system note is time-sensitive and must be rechecked if publication is delayed.

## New York

- [Sales tax registration](https://www.tax.ny.gov/pubs_and_bulls/tg_bulletins/st/do_i_need_to_register_for_sales_tax.htm): official content inspected; 20-day advance application applies when registration is required, not universally to all businesses.
- [General business records](https://www.tax.ny.gov/bus/doingbus/recordkeeping.htm) and [sales-tax vendor records](https://www.tax.ny.gov/pubs_and_bulls/tg_bulletins/st/record-keeping_requirements_for_sales_tax_vendors.htm): inspected; vendor bulletin opened successfully. Supports transaction taxability, certificate/locality evidence, purchase documentation and three-year timing with longer holds. The vendor bulletin bears a June 2, 2011 issue/update date; it remains on the current official site, but that age is a reason for pre-release reviewer confirmation.
- [Withholding filing requirements](https://www.tax.ny.gov/bus/wt/filing_requirements.htm): opened successfully; quarterly NYS-45, electronic filing, and state/NYC/Yonkers withholding where applicable. Current page flags March 2025 reporting changes; old formats are not assumed current.
- [WCB coverage requirements](https://www.wcb.ny.gov/content/main/coverage-required-db.jsp): official content inspected; disability/PFL coverage facts and exceptions.
- [New York paid sick leave](https://www.ny.gov/new-york-paid-sick-leave/new-york-paid-sick-leave): opened successfully; six-year payroll records including weekly sick-leave accrual/use. This is not described as a sales tax retention period.
- [Corporate-tax overview](https://www.tax.ny.gov/data/stats/taxfacts/corporation-tax.htm): official content inspected for tax-base and potential metropolitan/NYC distinctions. No rate repeated.

Unresolved: payroll, local withholding, entity and industry applicability need qualified review. A guessed DOL employer-handbook route failed; no claim relies on it. The six-year statement is supported by the successfully opened official sick-leave page, not that failed route.

## Washington

- [B&O return instructions](https://dor.wa.gov/file-pay-taxes/file-or-amend-my-return/instructions-completing-combined-excise-tax-return/combined-excise-tax-return-state-business-and-occupation-tax): official content inspected; gross-receipts basis and no general deductions for ordinary costs. The draft still acknowledges specific statutory deductions/credits.
- [ESD quarterly reports](https://esd.wa.gov/employer-requirements/quarterly-reports/how-file-your-quarterly-tax-and-wage-reports) and [Paid Leave reporting](https://paidleave.wa.gov/reporting/): inspected; unemployment and combined Paid Leave/WA Cares reports remain distinguishable.
- [Services newly subject to sales tax](https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/retail-sales-tax/services-newly-subject-retail-sales-tax): current official content inspected. October 1, 2025 expansion, July 1, 2026 exclusions/exemptions and ongoing litigation are all acknowledged. [September 12, 2025 interim DAS guidance](https://dor.wa.gov/laws-rules/interim_guidance_statements/interim-guidance-statement-regarding-changes-made-essb-5814-das-exclusions-and-definition-retail) supports caution about digital/data-processing scope. The pilot makes no ruling on Paper Trail.
- [WAC 458-20-254](https://app.leg.wa.gov/wac/default.aspx?cite=458-20-254): official Legislature content inspected; five-year DOR tax-record rule and interpretable electronic evidence. Two attempted DOR export/guessed recordkeeping URLs failed; the Legislature rule supplies the cited authority instead.
- [L&I payroll/personnel records](https://lni.wa.gov/workers-rights/workplace-policies/payroll-and-personnel-records) and [L&I business recordkeeping](https://www.lni.wa.gov/agency/small-business/recordkeeping-requirements): inspected; distinguish the payroll minimum three years from the separate current-plus-three-prior-years guidance for covered business/industrial-insurance records.

Unresolved: ongoing ESSB 5814 litigation and changing guidance require another check immediately before approval. Municipal B&O, capital-gains tax and future changes are outside the pilot; no broad 'Washington has no income tax' assertion appears. Tax classification, benefit coverage and worker status require qualified review.

## Publication And Expansion Gates

1. Owner approves the five-pilot design, practical depth and English-only scope.
2. A qualified state tax/payroll reviewer checks applicability and current sources, especially the recent Florida and Washington changes; record the real reviewer and date only after completion.
3. Verify rendered inline source links, local internal links, unique metadata, truthful canonical/breadcrumb/schema and no unsupported Q&A claims. Keep local drafts `noindex` and out of the sitemap.
4. Confirm responsive layout and readable source/example treatment on desktop, tablet and mobile. Text authored here is not evidence that a rendered page was visually tested.
5. Only after approval may actual published routes be linked from flags and included in the sitemap. Prepare the other 45 state research entries now; do not turn them into mass-generated pages before the quality gate.

Duplicate/doorway risk: the pilots use different state obligations and examples, rather than substituting a state name into identical tax text. Shared reconciliation principles are clearly labeled general practice. Expansion must preserve this distinction and add independently verified useful state content. No claim of an office, in-person team or local client is created.
