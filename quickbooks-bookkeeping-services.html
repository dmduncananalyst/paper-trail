QuickBooks Bookkeeping Services

Keep your bookkeeping organized in the QuickBooks system your business already uses. Paper Trail works with your financial records to review transactions, reconcile accounts, track money coming in and going out, and prepare reports that show where your business stands.

Whether you need ongoing bookkeeping or have transactions that require review and correction, we can evaluate your QuickBooks records and recommend the appropriate Paper Trail service. You maintain control of your business and financial decisions while we handle the bookkeeping work included in your agreement.

Bookkeeping in Your Existing QuickBooks Account

If your business uses QuickBooks Online, you do not need to start over with a different bookkeeping system just to work with Paper Trail. We work in your existing account using approved access and the records you provide. The work we perform depends on your QuickBooks subscription, the information available, and the services you select.

Your business retains ownership of its QuickBooks account, subscription, and financial records. QuickBooks software charges are separate from Paper Trail service fees.

What Our QuickBooks Bookkeeping Service Covers

Transaction Review and Categorization

We review recorded income and expenses, identify transactions that need clarification, and categorize activity according to the information you approve. Consistent transaction records make it easier to understand what the business receives, spends, and owes.

Bank and Credit Card Reconciliation

We compare the transactions recorded in QuickBooks with available bank and credit card statements, investigate differences, and work through reconciliation issues. Missing records, duplicate entries, or prior-period problems may require additional review or a separate bookkeeping repair engagement.

Income and Expense Tracking

We organize income and expense activity so that your records distinguish business revenue, operating costs, and other relevant transactions. You can review how money moves through your business without relying on bank balances alone.

Financial Reports

We prepare and review the financial reports included in your service agreement, such as the Profit and Loss and Balance Sheet reports, using the information recorded in QuickBooks. We flag items that require your confirmation so the reports reflect the available business records.

Accounts, Invoices, and Bills

Where included in your selected service, we can assist with tracking invoices, customer payments, bills, and vendor balances in QuickBooks. Detailed Accounts & Billing work is provided under the applicable Paper Trail service agreement and is not automatically included in every bookkeeping package.

Tax-Ready Bookkeeping Records

We organize the bookkeeping records and available financial reports for your tax preparer. Paper Trail does not automatically provide tax return preparation, tax filing, or tax advice as part of this service.

Weekly, Monthly, and Bookkeeping Repair Options

The frequency and scope of your QuickBooks bookkeeping depend on what your business needs. Paper Trail offers Weekly Bookkeeping for businesses requiring more frequent record review, Monthly Bookkeeping for a recurring monthly process, and Bookkeeping Repair when previous records are incomplete or need correction.

QuickBooks Bookkeeping Services is not an additional automatic charge or a replacement for those existing packages. We identify the applicable service and provide the appropriate quote or package information before work begins.

Working With Your QuickBooks Records

We begin by reviewing your existing QuickBooks setup and the condition of your records. You provide authorized account access and the financial information needed for the agreed work, including bank statements, transaction explanations, invoices, receipts, and other supporting documents when required.

We identify the bookkeeping tasks included in your service, confirm your review and approval responsibilities, and establish the schedule for recordkeeping and financial reporting. You remain responsible for providing complete, accurate information and approving business decisions, payments, and changes that require your authorization.

Your QuickBooks Account and Access

For QuickBooks Online, authorized company administrators can grant outside bookkeeping professionals access through the account's user management options. Access methods and available permissions depend on the QuickBooks product and current Intuit account setup. We confirm the appropriate access arrangement during onboarding.

You should never provide your personal QuickBooks password as part of an ordinary consultation. Access should be granted through the platform's supported user or professional invitation process.

QuickBooks Bookkeeping for Businesses at Different Stages

A business with current records may need regular categorization, reconciliations, and reports. Another business may have months of unreviewed transactions, unclear balances, or accounts that do not match statements. Those situations require different work and should not be treated as identical monthly bookkeeping assignments.

Paper Trail reviews the scope before confirming services. If your books require correction before recurring maintenance can begin, we can discuss Bookkeeping Repair separately.

Request QuickBooks Bookkeeping Services

Tell us what QuickBooks product your business uses, how current your records are, and whether you need weekly bookkeeping, monthly bookkeeping, or help correcting prior transactions. We will review your needs and explain the available Paper Trail service options.
